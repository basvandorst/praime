Puzzle 48.- P^3 = a^3 + b^3 + c^3, {P, a, b, c} = primes

Highly stimulated by the Russo's question (See Puzzle 47), I asked myself for the solution to cube of primes such that all the three cubes to be added are primes also (this is permitted by the parity involved).

The least solution that I have gotten is:

709^3 = 193^3 + 461^3 + 631^3

On the other hand, the first solution for P=palprime and {a,b,c} = odds is:

929^3 = 69^3 + 447^3 + 893^3 .

And is not very hard to find other solutions of this kind but…

a) does exists a solution being P a palprime and {a,b,c} = primes?

b) does exists a solution being P a palindrome and {a,b,c} = primes?

Felice Russo has found two solutions to b):

505^3=59^3+163^3+499^3 ..........(4/5/99)

535^3=349^3+ 379^3+383^3 ........(15/5/99)
