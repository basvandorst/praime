Problem 9.- Let f(n) be the number of ways of representing n (n can be a composite or a prime number) as the sum of one or more consecutive primes. For example :

f(0)=0, f(1)=0, f(2)=1, f(3)=1, f(4)=0, f(5)=2, f(6)=0, f(7)=1, f(8)=1,…f(41)=3

5 : 5=2+3

41 : 41=2+3+5+7+11+13 =11+13+17
