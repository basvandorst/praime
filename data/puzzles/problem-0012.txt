Problem 12.- Prime producing polynomials

Ruby found that the absolute value of (36X^2-810X +2753) assumes 45 distinct prime values for the 45 integers X, 0<=X<=44.

This the current record for this kind of polynomials (aX^2 + b.X +c)

Can you get a better polynomial than this?

Notes :
Williams noted that the polynomial 36X^2-2358X+36809 produces the same distinct prime values than the Ruby’s polynomial, but appearing in reverse order

Ruby and Fung have the second record polynomial, 47X^2 -1701X+10181 which produces 43 distinct prime values for 0<=X<=42

(Ref.1, P. 203)
