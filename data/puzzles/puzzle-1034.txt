Puzzle 1034. P+6k=Q, etc

JM Bergot sent the following nice puzzle:

11+6=17

17+12=29

29+18=47

47+24=71

71+30=101

101+36=137

137+42=179

179+48=227

227+54=281

2810+60=341 FAIL

This is a chain of nine successes.
Starting with any prime and any multiple of 6, can you find a longer chain with increasing multiples of 6? An example, 11+18=29, 29+24=53, 53+30=83, 83+36=119 FAIL.

Carlos Rivera, in a fast search found a chain of 21 terms: 23+6=29,...,1286+126=1409. Next term fails.

Later Bergot discovered that my improved solution was already published here.

Q. Find a larger chain starting with any prime and any multiple of 6.
