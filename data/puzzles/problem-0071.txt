Problem 71. n & pi(n) such that...

G. L. Honaker, Jr. sent the following nice puzzle:

Find the smallest number n such that n and (n) together contain each of the ten decimal digits exactly thrice.

Related solutions:

Once: (102756) = 9834, by G.L. Honaker, Jr. This is the only once-pandigital solution.

Twice: (10013236879) = 455627894, by Chuck Gaydos. This is the smallest twice-pandigital.

Contribution came from Giovanni Resta
