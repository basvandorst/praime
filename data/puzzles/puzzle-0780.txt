Puzzle 780. A second follow-up to Puzzle 776

Here we will ask for chain of primes
p1->p2->...

following the basic rule of puzzle 776:

p_i+1=p_i+Σ(d_j^2)p_i.

Carlos Rivera found a chain composed by 7 primes:

k=7:

2373457267→ 2373457517→ 2373457753→ 2373457997→ 2373458369→ 2373458671→ 2373458933

Q. Send your largest chain.
