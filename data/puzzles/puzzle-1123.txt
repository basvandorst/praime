Puzzle 1123 Tetractys

On Jan 29, 2023, G.L. Honaker, Jr. sent the following nice puzzle, related to a figure named "Tetractys":

The 15 primes in the above figure are: 2, 3, 5, 7, 13, 31, 37, 41, 43, 47, 59, 67, 73, 89 & 631

In later emails he added, "First maximum (24 primes) found by a student of mine. Chris Caldwell confirmed and found the other 8, but they are lost."

Q. Can you find these supposed 9 distinct maximum lost solutions, each one having 24 distinct primes (disregarding rotations and reflections), reading only over straight lines?

During the week 18-24 Feb. 2023, all of the following puzzles found the same 9 distinct asked solutions: Giorgos Kalogeropoulos, Michael Branicky, Oscar Volpatti, Paul Cleary, Emmanuel Vantieghem, J-M Rebert, Gennady Gusev, J_ Rebert, Michael Hürt
