Puzzle 1055. Google Prime Number

Claudio Meller sent the following nice link, where it is stated:

"379009, es el número primo de Google".

The same has been stated by Earls this way in a curio:

379009 is A prime number that spells Google when turned upside down on a calculator. [Earls]

Claudio's questions are:

Q. Qué otras páginas, países, palabras, etc tienen está característica??

Contributions came from Ivan Ianakiev, Emmanuel Vantieghem

***

Ivan wrote:

For the replacement rule {"0" -> "o", "3" -> "e", "5" -> "s", "7" -> "l", "9" -> "g"}

1. The English numbers/words are

{3,5,7,37,53,59,97,337,509,733,739,773,907,937,3907,5309,5507,35339,53507,55079,379009,379909}

{e,s,l,le,es,gs,lg,lee,gos,eel,gel,ell,log,leg,loge,goes,loss,geese,loses,gloss,google,goggle}

2. The Spanish numbers/words are

{3,37,53,73,307,337,353,373,709,739,37337,50773,53507,53773,5050709}

{"e", "le", "es", "el", "loe", "lee", "ese", "ele", "gol", "gel", "leele", "ellos", "loses", "elles", "golosos"}

3. My algorithm generates only the numbers that are prime and consisting of the digits {0,3,5,7,9} and checks whether the corresponding words are valid. Another possible approach is to convert every word in the dictionary into a number and check whether it is prime and consisting of the digits mentioned, but I think it will take a lot of time and effort to check every single word in the dictionary.
