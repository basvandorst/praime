Puzzle 972. Three questions about Primevals

Carlos Rivera asks this:

1) Find a larger prime primeval integer producing a prime quantity of primes, with a prime index (10034579, 4517, 47^th )

2) Find a larger prime primeval integer producing a prime quantity of primes (10123579, 5953, 50^th )

3) Find a larger prime primeval integer (100123456789, 13834293, 98^th)
