Puzzle 1065 A larger integer than 45 such that...

JM Bergot sent the following nice puzzle:

The sum of the divisors of 45 is 78, with divisors 1, 3, 5, 9, 15, 45.

78mod1=0

78mod3=0

78mod5=3

78mod9=6

78mid15=3

78mod45=33

Add the remainders 0+0+3+6+3+33=45.

Q. Can you find a larger number than 45 with this property?
