Puzzle 348. 414347 prime string

Peter Sysel posed the following nice puzzle:

Three subsequent primes (41, 43 & 47) in their concatenated form (414347) - and its evenly decomposed sums 414+347, 41+43+47, 4+1+4+3+4+7 are all primes.

Question: What is the next prime string with the same property?
