Puzzle 775. Triangles and consecutive primes

Vic Bold sent the following nice puzzle:

Let p1, p2, p3, p4, p5 & p6 to be six consecutive primes in the same order.

Let draw a triangle having the following vertexes: (p1, p2), (p3, p4) & (p5, p6).

Let A to be the surface of this triangle.

We are now looking for primes sextets such that:

a) A is a square (example for p1=2520552823, A=36864,

sqrt(A)=192)
b) A=2^a (example for p1=4085747551, a=15)

Q1: Report your prime sextet such that A is your largest square.

Q2: Report your prime sextet such that A is your largest a.

Contributions came from Flavio Torasso, Emmanuel Vantieghem
