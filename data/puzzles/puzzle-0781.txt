Puzzle 781. A second follow-up to Puzzle 776

Here we will ask for primes p that generate k primes
p→ {p1, p2, ...pi... pk}

following a variation of the basic rule of puzzle 776:

p_i=p+Σ(d_j^i)_p, for i=1 to k

Carlos Rivera found a prime that generates 10 primes:

k=10, p=2237051

2237051→ {2237071, 2237143, 2237563, 2240191, 2257291, 2371183, 3141163, 8399551, 44564491, 294539023}
2237071 = 2237051+ 2^1+2^1+3^1+7^1+0^1+5^1+1^1

2237143 = 2237051+ 2^2+2^2+3^2+7^2+0^2+5^2+1^2

...

294539023 = 2237051+ 2^10+2^10+3^10+7^10+0^10+5^10+1^10

Q. Send your prime p with the largest set of k>10 primes thus generated.
