Puzzle 147. Twin & reversible primes

The first non trivial example of twin & reversible primes is:

(71, 73) & (17, 37)

A somewhat larger example is:

(173313197, 173313199) & (791313371, 991313371)

One more example I have calculated for this puzzle is:

(10^60+68599471, 10^60+68599473) & (17499586*10^53+1, 37499586*10^53+1)

Question:

Calculate a) a semi-titanic couple b) a titanic couple

Jim Fougeron found (11/1/2002) larger examples of the asked pairs, still not a semi-titanic (500 digits) but large enough to make a record by the moment:

Sub titanic twin/reversible pair of primes:

244909752*809#-1 & 244909752*809#+1 and their reverses. These are

all 341 digits in length. They have been proven. The primorials

using N+1 and N-1 proving techniques, and the reverses by using

ECPP with Primo.
