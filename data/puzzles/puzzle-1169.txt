Davide Rotondo, sent the following puzzle.

Strange prime Producing Polynomial

f(n) = n^2-76*n+1607 produces 78 successive prime integers, for n=0 to77 if for each odd n f(n) must be divided by 4.

1607,383,1459,347,1319,313,1187,281,1063,251,947,223,839,197,739,173,647,151,563,131,487,113,419,97,359,

83,307,71,263,61,227,53,199,47,179,43,167,41,163,41,167,43,179,47,199,53,227,61,263,71,307,83,359,97,419,

113,487,131,563,151,647,173,739,197,839,223,947,251,1063,281,1187,313,1319,347,1459,383,1607,421
