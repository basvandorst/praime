Puzzle 965. Partitioning an 8x8 square

Fausto Morales sent the following nice puzzle:

Find the unique partition of the 8x8 square below into the 10 smallest primes containing digits 1 and 9 only, so that each prime appears once left-to-right and once top-to-bottom.

1 9 9 9 1 9 9 1

9 1 1 1 1 9 1 9

1 1 9 1 1 1 1 9

9 9 1 1 1 1 1 9

9 1 9 9 1 9 1 1

9 9 1 9 1 9 9 9

1 9 9 1 9 9 1 1

1 9 1 9 1 9 9 9

The 10 smallest primes mentioned above are;

11, 19, 191, 199, 911, 919, 991, 1999, 9199, 11119

Q1. Send your partition-solution.

A solved example:

If the 8x8 matriz is:

1 9 1 9 9 9 1 1

9 1 1 9 1 9 9 9

9 1 9 1 9 1 9 1

9 1 1 1 9 9 1 9

1 1 1 1 1 1 9 1

9 9 1 1 9 9 9 9

9 1 1 1 1 9 1 9

1 9 9 9 1 9 1 9

The solution is this one:

1
9 | 1 | 9 | 9 | 9 | 1 | 1

9
1 | 1 | 9 | 1 | 9 | 9 | 9

9
1 | 9 | 1 | 9 | 1 | 9 | 1

9
1 | 1 | 1 | 9 | 9 | 1 | 9

1
1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 9 | 1

9
9 | 1 | 1 | 9 | 9 | 9 | 9

9
1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 9 | 1 | 9

1
9 | 9 | 9 | 1 | 9 | 1 | 9
