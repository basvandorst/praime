Puzzle 1172  Follow up to Puzzle 1171

Paolo Lava sent the following nice puzzle as a follow up to Puzzle 1171

Let p a prime with k digits (d_1, d_2,…,d_k). For any digit, delete it from p. Take the sum of these numbers and check if it is a prime. 
 

Q. Are there chains of 8 or more primes we can get reiterating this process ?
 

First chains with 7 primes for  17577607,  22977607, 36992723, 65794423, 71564809, 90272807, ...
