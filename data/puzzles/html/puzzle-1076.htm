Puzzle 1076 Set of integers N, 1<N<S such that...

 
Giorgos Kalogeropoulos, sent the following interesting puzzle:

For every positive integer N with  1 < N < S, we are interested in those N such that when N^2 is divided by S, leaves a remainder 1.

These N form a list {N1,N2,...Nk}. We are searching for S such that all terms of the list are primes.
e.g. S=8 -> {3,5,7} list of length 3 and all terms are primes.
       S=24 -> {5,7,11,13,17,19,23} length 7 and all terms are primes.
       S=123450 -> {19751,41149,60901,62549,82301,103699,123449} length 7 and all terms are primes.

 
Q1. What is the largest S of length 7 that you can find?
Q2. Can you find an S of length greater than 7 or prove that it does not exist?

During the week 20-26 Feb, 2022, contributions came from Paul Cleary, Emmanuel Vantieghem, Michael Hürter, Gennady Gusev, Adam Stinchcombe
