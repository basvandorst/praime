Emmanuel wrote:

First of all, the numbers  30984360, 215470920  and  349764576 in your list are not a sum of two consecutive primes.

The sequence should be 39851304, 414750412.
It continues with  945210480 (sum of 2, 4, 6, 8, 10  but not  12  CPs).
The next is  1622295444  and is also the sum of  12  CPs.
I continued the search for numbers that are sum of  2k  CPs with  k = 1, 2, ..., 6  and I found :
  31837620450, 67464295522, 233805285570, 370398556558, 960387691512, 978423084312.
None of them is the sum of  14  CPs.  The first such number is certainly > 1065846890418.
But I guess it will be much bigger.
I came to this by looking at the smallest number  m(n)  that is sum of  2, 4, ..., 2n  CPs :
m(1) = 8
m(2) = 36
m(3) = 8412
m(4) = 227304
m(5) = 39851304
m(6) = 1622295444,
so, the next one may have 14  digits.
