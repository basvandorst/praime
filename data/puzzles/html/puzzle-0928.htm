Puzzle 928. Surreal integers

Fausto Morales sent the following nice puzzle.

A surreal number is a positive integer resulting from collating the numbers of an equivalent arithmetical expression, in order of appearance, using common auxiliary arithmetical symbols and operators.

Examples:

713 = -7•1 + (3!)!

715 = (7-1)! - 5

Q1: Can you find more surreal numbers?

A surreal prime is a prime surreal number, such as:

127 = -1 + 2^7

Q2: Can you find more surreal primes?
