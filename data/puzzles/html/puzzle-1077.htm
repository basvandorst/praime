Puzzle 1077 These numbers that are...

Zak Seidov sent the following nice puzzle

Some numbers that are  sum of m=3 consecutive primes and also product of 3 consecutive primes:
{33263, 7566179, 10681031, 29884301}.

Example:
33263 = 11083 + 11087 + 11093 = 29*31*37.
