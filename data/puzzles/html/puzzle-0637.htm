Puzzle 637. Factorials as product of palindromes

As a follow up of Puzzle 636 now we ask for these factorials that are expressible a a product of two or more palindromes (two or more digits, please).

Giovanni Resta sent one example:

15! = 88 * 525 * 585 * 48384

Q. Can you find more examples (please just send your smallest & your largest)?
