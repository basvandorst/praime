Problem 5.- Missed prime factors in Woodall numbers

A Woodall number is of the following form : n*2n -1

The smallest Woodall numbers with unknown prime factors are :

368*2368 -1 = 29*3233254457712259*C97
388*2388 -1=  3*57389*21883688258780797*C98
395*2395 -1=  3*11*587*8467*648624223293259*C99
409*2409 -1 = 11*311*541*49333*5999187334683223*C99

would you like to try to search for those unknown prime factors hidden
in the composites C97, C98, & C99’s ?

***
