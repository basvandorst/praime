Puzzle 666 Columns = Rows

Recently I started playing with the following square objects, of which I got without too much effort (because I used an elemental code) the following examples:

Example1:

1013
0191
1933
3137

where -as you can immediately see:

a) each row(i) is equal to the column(i)
b) the sum of the first n-1 rows is equal to the row(n)
c) less evident, each row (and consequently each column) is a prime number

Here is a larger one.

Example 2:

111227
100003
106591
205019
209159
731999

Q. Send your largest example.
