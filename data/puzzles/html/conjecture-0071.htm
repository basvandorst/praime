Conjecture 71. Twin primes and π.

Werner Sand sent the following conjecture:

Product[1/(1-1/t)] (t=3...infinite) = π = 3.14159...

According to computations, the product increases monotonously from 3/2 to  π and at the point of t = 23887065569, it is = 3.024374573...

Q. What can you comment about this conjecture?
