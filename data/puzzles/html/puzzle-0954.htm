Puzzle 954. Growing by the center, sequence of primes

Carlos Rivera poses the following puzzle:

Here we deal with sequence of primes that grow for the center, two digits xy at a time, without repeating these xy values.

Example: Starting with the two digits prime: "11"

11
1021
100621
10012621
...

where the inserted xy digits are: 02, 06, 12, and so on...

My largest sequence is one starting with the two digits prime 31, and finishing with a 118 digits prime:

39999889999348944532221473565546713568117208260779315204232452113850013
24767179248444034591098680567392557391849262731

Q. Send your largest solution.

Giovanni wrote on May 23, 2019

The largest prime I found for Puzzle 954 has 130 digits:

300010000809569664782543135217163618996158473794355134838108764835700
7889334904744162664560321024985086526833863896328591762174301.

The search space is so large that I just scratched it, so I'm rather 
confident there are much longer solutions.

I've also investigated the restricted case in which
all the "centers" we add have to be distinct 2-digits primes (11, 13, 
..., 97),

In this case it is easy to explore all the possibilities, and the 
largest prime is 85356231189279373911973799.

If we allow repetitions of the "centers", then we can reach 
411838643875146272183193393119993777379311.

If we allow also 02, 03, 05, 07, as the centers then the maximal values 
are 3309271405676812739397119337937271 and 
5277943160464386195600450673933513771971113111771333, respectively.
