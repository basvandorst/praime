Puzzle 1061 Consecutive numbers and the quantity of distinct prime factors

Metin Sariyar sent the following nice puzzle:

Find the smallest number k such that number of distinct prime divisors of exactly n numbers from k to k+n-1 increase by 1, while it decreases by 1 for their reversals.

The first 3 terms, for n=2, 3 & 4, I found are: 9, 13683, 1697257, ...?
