Puzzle 863. 2017 a new prime year

Emmanuel Vantieghem sent the following new year greetings:

2017 = 2*3*5 - 7 + 11*13*17 - 19*23, a nice arithmetic curio using ordered the first 9 primes.

I have obtained this other curio, 2017 =2*7*11*13+3*5, using disordered the first 6 primes.

Q1. Send your own nice curio for 2017 using only prime numbers.

Digging around I found the following interesting other properties for 2017:

  • 2017=9^2+44^2 (Fermat's 4n+1 theorem)
  • 2017 is a pancake number, because a pancake can be divided into 2017 parts by 63 straight cuts. 2017=(63^2+63+2)/2.... (Numbers Aplenty, Giovanni Resta)
  • 2017=1^3+3^3+4^3+5^3+6^3+7^3+8^3+9^3 (Claudio Meller)
  • 2017 = 1331 + 686 (Claudio Meller)

Q2. Send more interesting arithmetic properties for 2017.

Jean Brette wrote on Jan 18, 2017:

2017 is a full reptend prime, i.e.  a prime number  p  such that the length of the period of  1/ p   is maximal and equal to (p-1). In this case , the period is a cyclic number. The smallest reptend prime is 7. The period of 1/7  is 142857.
