Puzzle 959. Integers equal to, both, the sum of...

Patrick Capelle found  (when?) this interesting curio:

The sum of the primes from 2 to 357198967 is equal to the prime number 3325997869054417, and this is also equal to the sum of the non-primes integers from 1 to 83961758.

Q1. Is there another larger solution, prime or not?
Q2. Are there smaller prime solutions?
