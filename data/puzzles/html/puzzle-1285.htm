Puzzle 1285 The Left-Crescendo Serpent

 Now we will permit the serpent of the Puzzle 1284,only grow to the left.

Except fot his change the serpent can start in any integer from 2 to 9. This left-crescendo can not start with the integer 1, for obvious reasons, and will end when the quantity of distinct prime factors is greater than 9.

For this kind of left-crescendo serpent, Carlos Rivera has computed the following maximum ones.
