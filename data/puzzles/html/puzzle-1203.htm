Puzzle 1203 2025

On Dec 127, 2024 Emmanuel Vantieghem wrote:

1) 2025 = (12-3-4)*(5*6+7+8)*9=(9*(8*7-6-5)*(4+(3-2)*1)

2) 2025 = (3^(11-7))* 5^2=(2+3)*5*((7-11+13)^(-17+19))
 
From some other places and communications I found also that:

3) 2025 = 45^2 = 5^2*9^2 = 5^2*3^4 = 1^6*3^4*5^2

4) 2025 = 40^2+20^2+5^2

5) 2025 = (1+2+...9)^2

6) 2025 = (20+25)^2

7) 2025 = 1^3+2^3+...+9^3 = (1+2+...9)^2

8) 2025 = 12×3+(4+5)×(6+7)×(8+9).

9) 2025 = 9×8+76+5^4×3+2×1.

Q. Perhaps you can add some other interesting expressions for 2025.
