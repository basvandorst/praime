Puzzle 979. Primes and sum of repunits

Carlos Rivera asks this:

If R(n) is a "Repunit" composed of n digits "1" then

S(R(n))* = R(1)+R(2)+…R(n) is prime for n=2497 and 3301.

Q. Find more n values such that S(R(n)) is prime?

________________
