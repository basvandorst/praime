Puzzle 668 Fortyfive

I saw recently this curio in the nice site "futility closet"

452 = 2025

20 + 25 = 45

453 = 91125

9 + 11 + 25 = 45

454 = 4100625

4 + 10 + 06 + 25 = 45

455 = 184528125

18 + 4 + 5 + 2 + 8 + 1 + 2 + 5 = 45

456 = 8303765625

8 + 3 + 0 + 3 + 7 + 6 + 5 + 6 + 2 + 5 = 45

 Q. Can you get another/better solution, prime or not?

Contributions came from Giovanni Resta, Jan van Delden & Hakan Summakoglu.
