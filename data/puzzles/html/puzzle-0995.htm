Puzzle 995. Another sequence of primes

Paolo Lava sent the following nice puzzle:

Take a prime p(i) and concatenate it with p(i+1). If the result is prime then add p(i)+p(i+1) and concatenate the sum to p(i+2). If the result is still prime then take p(i)+p(i+1)+p(i+2) and concatenate it to p(i+3) and so on.

Here the list of the minimum prime that holds this process for n step:

n    p
1    2
2    31
3    331
4    832757
5    2683591
