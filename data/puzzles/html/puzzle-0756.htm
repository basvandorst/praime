Puzzle 756. Primonacci

Dimitry Kamenetsky sent the following nice puzzle.

 Let S be a sequence of prime numbers such that S(1)=a,
S(2)=b, S(n+1)=S(n)+S(n-1)+c, where a<b and c=odd>0, for 1<=n<=k

 

Here is my best solution for this puzzle. It is a sequence with length 19:

10331, 67073, 109619, 208907, 350741, 591863, 974819, 1598897, 2605931, 4237043, 6875189, 11144447, 18051851, 29228513, 47312579, 76573307, 123918101, 200523623, 324473939

Q. Find a larger sequence like this.
