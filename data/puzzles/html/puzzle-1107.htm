Puzzle 1107 Place 11 after its base result

JM Bergot sent the following nice puzzle, discovered by Robert Israel:
I place 11 after its base result:
 
Base 2: 11=1011 and 101111 is prime.
Base 3: 11=102 and  10211 is prime.
Base 4: 11=23 and 2311 is prime.
Base 5: 11=21 and 2111 is prime.
Base 6: 11=15 and 1511 is prime.
Base 7: 11=14 and 1411=17*83.
Q. Is there a prime that will work for bases
2 to 7 or more?
