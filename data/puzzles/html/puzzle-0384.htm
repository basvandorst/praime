Puzzle 384. Prime Reverse Keith numbers

For sure you already know what a Keith number (KN) is.

Then you know what is a Reverse Keith number (RKN), too. Here (A097060) you can find the RKN already known, as calculated by Jason Earls in Set. 2004:

12, 24, 36, 48, 52, 71, 341, 682, 1285, 5532, 8166, 17593, 28421, 74733, 90711, 759664, 901921, 1593583, 4808691, 6615651, 6738984, 8366363, 8422611

From these 23 RKN, only 71 & 1593583 are primes.

Questions

1. Can you extend the sequence A097060 ?

2. Can you extend it as far as to find two more primes in it?
 

Contributions came from B. Boncompagni, Antoine Verroken, Jacques Tramu & Anton Vrba who got the largest extension to the existing sequence.
