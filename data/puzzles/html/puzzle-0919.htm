Puzzle 919. K csc primes without certain ending digit

Vic Bold sent the following nice puzzle.

From 3779 to 3889 there are 12 prime numbers not ending
in digit 9.  Is there a longer list missing one last digit

By my side I (CR) found a larger set:

from 2462372461 to 2462373707 there are 57 consecutive primes not ending in the digit "3".
