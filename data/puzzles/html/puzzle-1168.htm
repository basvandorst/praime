Puzzle 1168  Primes for the Baxter-Hickerson function

G. L. Honaker, Jr. sent the following challenging puzzle.

The Baxter-Hickerson function f(k) = (2*10^(5*k) - 10^(4*k) + 2*10^(3*k) + 10^(2*k) + 10^k + 1)/3 has the property that its cubes lack of zeros.

On top of that, it has been found that f(k) is prime for k=0, 1, 7, 133.
