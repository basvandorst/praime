Puzzle 403. 833021343

Farideh Firoozbakht wrote:

833021343 is the smallest natural number such that 833021343+4^k is prime for 0<k<16.

Question: Find the smallest natural number n such that n+4^k is prime for 0<k<=z, z=>16

 
 

Contributions came from J. K. Andersen, Bernardo Boncompagni & Fred Schneider

***

Andersen wrote:

The puzzle asks for 0<k<z, z=>16. The given solution n=833021343 is the
smallest for z=16 (maybe the intended question was 0<k<=z ?).
The first three with z>16 all end at z=17 and are: 12465115083 (11 digits),
2735670562857 (13 digits), 83003201588773 (14 digits). The first of these is
surprisingly small; around 1/6659 of the third.
The smallest n for z = 2 to 19 is:
1, 1, 3, 7, 7, 15, 37, 163, 177, 4503, 4503, 4503, 4503, 4503, 833021343,
12465115083, 95854279610863, 1158174141556287.
I will submit the extension to
http://www.research.att.com/~njas/sequences/A130003.
 

***

Bernardo wrote:

12465115083 yields k=16. I have not computed the chance for a given
number of n digit to be in the sequence, but I suspect it drops to zero
quickly enough to expect only a finite number of solutions. I was
actually already surprised to find the solution for k=16 so relatively
close to the one by Farideh. There are no better solutions below 284*10^9.
