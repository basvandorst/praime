Puzzle 1264 A follow up to Puzzle 973

In 2009, J. K. Andersen got the following twin & empirps:

10^499+X+/-1 for X=174295123052

 10^499+174295123052 +1 is prime
10^499+174295123052 -1 is prime
Rev(10^499+174295123052 +1) is prime
Rev(10^499+174295123052 -1) is prime

(The four 500 digits primes verified by CR using ECM)
Perhaps it's time to get something larger than this.
Q.  Find larger examples of these kind
