Puzzle 426. q = p +rad(p+1)

Juan López González noticed that:

Starting with the prime p1=3 and
Using the recurrence relationship:

pi+1 = pi + rad(pi +1)
where
rad(n) = Π(q), for every q|n

Then every pi is prime for i=1 to 8, while p9 is composite.

3 + rad(4) = 5 is prime
5 + rad(6) = 11 is prime
11 + rad(12) = 17 is prime
17 + rad(18) = 23 is prime
23 + rad(24) = 29 is prime
29 + rad(30) = 59 is prime
59 + rad(60) = 89 is prime
and
89 + rad(90) = 119 = 7 · 17 is not prime.

He asks for other primes p1 with larger chain of primes.
