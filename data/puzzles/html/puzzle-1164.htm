Puzzle 1164  181601808106181
As a follow up from previous Puzzle 1163, both Oscar Volpatti & Emmanuel Vantieghem noticed the following solution:

181601808106181 -> 181901808109181 = 101 * 1801008001081

Oscar added: "In these cases, both factors of resulting semiprime are tetradic primes."

As a matter of fact, Oscar found a second and larger example:

10608808080880601 -> 10908808080880901 =
101 *108008000800801

Q. Find more examples like these two


 

From Feb 24 to March 1, contributions came from Michael Branicky, PaulCleary, Giorgos Kalogeropoulos, JM Rebert, Emmanuel Vantieghem, Oscar Volpatti

A titanic solution with 1035 digits can be obtained as follows.
Build number S by concatenating:
114 copies of composite palindrome "1991",
9 zeros,

9 zeros again,
