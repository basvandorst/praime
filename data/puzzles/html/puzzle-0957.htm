Puzzle 957. Largest prime with pandigital ordered expressions

Carlos Rivera asks this:

Find large primes as arithmetic expressions using just one of each of the following integers in rigorous order:

a)       1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9

b)      1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9,0

c)       9,8,7,6,5,4,3,2,1

d)      9,8,7,6,5,4,3,2,1,0

and using only the following five arithmetical operators +, -, *, /, ^ and parentheses "(" and ")" as auxiliary symbols.

Examples:

a)       1+2^3+4^5+6^(7+8+9) = 4738381338321617929 (19 digits, prime!)

b)      1+2^3+4^5+6^7*8^90 = 5310771084122086847626078987778794811033679696258
78641622214217512372958967973873189897 (87 digits, prime!)

c)       9^87-6^(5-4)+3^2+1 = 10449567633177831596610387890345070198960878107
3244439950619431748912396904023371773 (84 digits, prime!)

d)      9^8*7^6*5^4*3-2+10 = 9495756897991883 (16 digits, prime!)

Q. Send your three largest primes for each type of expression.
