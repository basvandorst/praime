Puzzle 1029. p that divides the number of partitions of p

On Dec. 21, 2020, G.L. Honaker, Jr. made me notice this interesting prime curio from Matin Sariyar:

The largest known prime p that divides P(p), the number of partitions of p. Note that P(5273) = 21324454654025779150078250115620526414565357199214419652174475578296382696055 = 5 × 7 × 17 × 37 × 5273 × 305971 × 182248 634924 894453 × 283809 003678 349559 × 11607 287812 587037 273754 619257. [Sariyar]

Q. Can you find another example?

 

During the week Jan 30 to Feb 5, contributions came from Oscar Volpatti, Simon Cavegn.

***

Oscar wrote:

I identified the first 17 numbers n that divide their partition number P(n):
1, 2, 3, 124, 158, 342, 693, 1896, 3853, 4434, 5273, 8640, 14850, 17928, 110516, 178984, 274534.
Primes are marked in red. I checked all integers up to 2*10^7 and all primes up to 1.4*10^8, finding no more solutions.
