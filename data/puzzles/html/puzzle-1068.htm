Puzzle 1068 Follow up to Puzzle 1067

Redo Puzzle 1067 considering the Rules of reading and counting the primes, but now:

Q1. For N=7, produce a solution with the maximal quantity of distinct primes considering the emirps

Q2. Produce a solution for the largest N value you can work obtaining at least 4N+4 distinct primes.

During the week 18-24 Dec 2021, contributions came from Giorgos Kalogeropoulos

***

Giorgos wrote:

Here is a 7x7 square that is an answer to Q1 and Q2: 

 
1 6 6 1 1 6 1
6 0 0 9 5 8 9
6 2 9 9 2 2 1
1 5 9 9 5 0 9
1 0 5 2 5 5 1
1 0 5 1 0 5 1
1 1 9 1 1 9 9

 
and its rotation

 
6 6 1 1 6 1 1
1 5 0 1 5 0 1
1 5 5 2 5 0 1
6 0 5 6 6 5 1
1 2 2 6 6 2 9
6 8 5 6 0 0 9
1 9 1 1 9 9 1

This square is of the form 4N+4 having 32 distinct primes as column, rows and diagonals.
Also, those squares contain 12 emirps:  {1501501,1552501,1525501,1826501,1666211,9266221,1051051,1055251,1052551,9059951,1111661,1129991}.
But we only care about emirps that have at least one 6 or 9 (in order to be distinct).
So, the total number of distinct primes is 38.
