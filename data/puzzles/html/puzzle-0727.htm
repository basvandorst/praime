Puzzle 727. Extending the OEIS sequence A235680

Here we produce a sequence from a starting prime integer using recursively the following rules:

1) the next term of the sequence is another prime number equal to the sum of the previous prime term plus the product of the non-zero digits plus the sum of the digits, of the previous prime term in both operations.

2) The sequence stops when the last term is a composite, not included in the sequence

Example:

Lets start with the prime = 127. The sequence has exactly 5 prime terms, the original one and four produced:

127, 151, 163, 191, 211, [217, composite]

As a matter of fact on Jan 13, 2014, I sent the following smallest initial terms for the OEIS seq A235680:

a(n): 2, 191, 163, 151, 127, 1644997, 36778597, 935715673,...

Almost immediately Giovanni Resta produced one more term: 50682890749.

Q. Can you find a few next terms in this sequence?
