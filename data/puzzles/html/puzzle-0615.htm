Puzzle 615. Abs[R(n/3)-n]<=1

Claudio Meller found that 783742161 and all the numbers of the form
(7425)n741, for n=>0, have the following property:

R(n/3)-n=1....(*)

Example: 741 is such that 741/3= 247, & R(247)=742...

I tried to get solutions to this more general property:

Abs[R(n/3)-n]<=1.................(**)

I only got one more solution to (**), other than the already reported by Meller, for n=42.

Q. Can you get other solutions to (**)?
