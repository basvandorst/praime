Puzzle 464. p+4.x2

Farideh sent the following nice puzzle:

The following sequence (A145741) of primes gives numbers p such that p+4*m^2
for m=0,1,2,...,10 are prime.

163, 409333, 1867783, 828497443, 2059599067, ?

Q1. What is the next term?

A very strange thing related to the first term that we found before (see the answer to puzzle 461) is : 163+4*m^2 for m=0,1,2,...,19 are prime (no other prime like 163 up to 3.10^10)

Q2. Is there another number like this one?
