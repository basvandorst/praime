Puzzle 711 Composites with the exactly the same digits than its prime factors

The smallest composite that has the same digits that its prime factors is 1255 because: 1225 = 5*251

As a matter of fact there are many examples of biprimes.

What is the smallest example of a composite with K prime factors for K=2, 3, 4,....

Here are my findings for K=2, 3, & 4

K Composite Prime factors
2 1255 5, 251
3 163797 3, 71, 769
4

38793587

 7, 73, 89, 853

Q. Send your smallest example for K>4.
