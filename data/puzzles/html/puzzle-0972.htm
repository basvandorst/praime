Puzzle 972. Three questions about Primevals

Carlos Rivera asks this:

1)      Find a larger prime primeval integer producing a prime quantity of primes, with a prime index (10034579, 4517, 47th )

2)      Find a larger prime primeval integer producing a prime quantity of primes (10123579, 5953, 50th )

3)      Find a larger prime primeval integer (100123456789, 13834293, 98th)
