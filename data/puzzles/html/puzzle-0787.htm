Puzzle 787. Fibonacci as sum of two consecutive primes.

Abhiram R. Devesh sent the following nice puzzle.

"Find Fibonacci numbers as sum of two consecutive prime numbers."

a(4) = 1548008755920 = 774004377953 + 774004377967 = fib(60)
