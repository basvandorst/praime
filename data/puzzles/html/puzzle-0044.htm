Puzzle 44.- “Enoch Haga Puzzle about Consecutive Primes”

A. Find sets of k consecutive odd primes such that:

P1 + Pk + 1 = prime
P2 + Pk-1 + 1 = prime
P3 + Pk-2 + 1 = prime
etc. 

Observe that when k is odd the central prime is simply ignored.
