Problem 10.- Find a 500-digit prime p and positive integers x, y

Find a 500-digit prime p and positive integers x, y

such that (p-1)/2 is prime, p divides 4^x - 9, and p divides 4^y - 25".

Bernstein says "I'll pay $100 for the first published example of p, x, y".

Problem posed by D. J. Bernstein (30/06/98), pointed out to me by mail by Samuli Larvala at 18/08/98.
