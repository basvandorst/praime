Puzzle 807. Symmetrical compositions of twin primes

Natalia Makarova sent the following nice puzzle:

We consider the consecutive twin primes

(p1, p1+2), … (p2, p2+2), …, (p3, p3+2), … , (pn, pn+2), where n > 2 and p1 < p2 <p3 <…< p 

Between the twin primes may be other primes for which we do not pay attention.

Then formed composition (p1, p2, p3, ..., pn), which should be symmetrical.

Required for each n > 2 find the composition with a minimal value of p1.

Examples:

n=3

(5, 7), (11, 13), (17, 19)

Symmetrical composition:

[5, 11, 17]

5+17=2*11

n=4

(29, 31), (41, 43), (59, 61), (71, 73)

Symmetrical composition:

[29, 41, 59, 71]

29+71=41+59
