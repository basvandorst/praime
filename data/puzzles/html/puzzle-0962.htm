Puzzle 962. Claudio Meller 1519 entry.

Claudio Meller in his entry 1519 ask for a larger solution than this one

     59 x 2 + 59 + 2 = 179 Primo 
59 x 3 + 59 + 3 =  239 Primo
59 x 5 + 59 + 5 =  359 Primo
59 x 7 + 59 + 7 =  479 Primo
59 x 11 + 59 + 11 = 719 Primo
59 x 13 + 59 + 13 = 839 Primo

 The fixed prime is 59 and the variable primes are 2, 3, ... 13

A larger solutions means here more than 6 rows. The variable primes need to be consecutive but not necessarily starting in 2.
