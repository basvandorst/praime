Puzzle 854. Follow up to Puzzle 853

Emmanuel Vantieghem sent the following nice follow up to Puzzle 853

There might be other polynomials, say  a x^2 +b x +c  with smaller coefficients (for instance, such that  a+b+c  is smallest) that are prime at 0 and 81  and composite in-between.

A good starting point is the following solution found by Emmanuel:

 x^2 + x + 16064431 is prime at  0  and  81  and composite in-between. So, a+b+c=16064433

Q. Find your a, b, c values for this question such that a+b+c<16064433.

Contributions came from Seiji Tomita, Jan van Delden, Shyam Sunder Gupta and Michael Hürter and Emmanuel Vantieghem
