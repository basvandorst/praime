          Puzzle 1216 Related to Puzzle 1212

In this puzzle I will ask to insert a well defined string in-between each pair of consecutve digits of an integer p, according to the rule described in the sequence A381732, (Puzzle 1212). After making all the possible insertions in p, you will have a new integer q.

Example:

p=1693 (prime)
q= 123456789876543 (composite)

Here are the first nine p values such that p & q are both prime integers:

p q
29 23456789
47 4567
73 76543
113 1123
239 23456789
269 23456789
353 34543
373 345676543
383 34567876543

In the table above I have used bold & blue characters only for these pairs (p, q) where both are palprimes

Q1. Find the first 20 pais (p, q) where both are palprimes

Q2. Send the largest q you can get where both p & q are palprimes.
