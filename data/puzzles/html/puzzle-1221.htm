          Puzzle 1221 A puzle about twin primes

 Long time ago G..L. Honaker, Jr. sent the followin curio & puzzle:

59# + 61 is prime (1922760350154212639131).
Note that {59, 61} is a twin prime pair.

Q. Find a all the examples alike you can find

From May 17 to 23, 2025, contributions came from Michael Branicky, Paul Cleary, Oscar Volpatti, Simon Cavegn,
