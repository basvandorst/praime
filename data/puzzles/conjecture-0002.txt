Conjecture 2. Chen's Conjecture

"Chen's work mentioned in the discussion of the Goldbach conjecture also showed that every even number is the difference between a prime and a P2." Note : P2 is "a number with no more than two prime factors"

(http://www.utm.edu/research/primes/notes/conjectures/#Goldbach)
