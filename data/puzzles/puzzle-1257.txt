Puzzle 1257 2^p-7, all composites if p is prime?

On Feb 4, 2026, Sebastián Martín Ruiz sent this interesting puzzle.

According to OEIS A059609*, 2^n-7 is prime for the following twelve known n
values: 39, 715, 1983, 2319, 2499, 3775, 12819, 63583, 121555, 121839, 468523 & 908739 **, . You know what? all of these twelve n values, are composite integers!

Q1. Can you find the next n value?

According to OEIS A059609, it´s valid suppose that 2^p-7 is composite for at least all p, prime numbers <= 908731 (the largest prime less than 908739).

Q2. Then, prove that for p=prime>2, 2^p-7 is composite or find a counterexample.

_________

* A059609 has a long story, covering 22 years: Author: Andrey V. Kulsha, Feb 02 2001. EXTENSIONS: a(8) from Henri Lifchitz; a(9)-a(10) from Gary Barnes, added by Max Alekseyev, Feb 09 2012; a(11) from Lelio R Paula, added by Max Alekseyev, Oct 25 2015; a(12) from Jon Grantham, Aug 09 2023.

** The integer 2^908739-7 has around 273558 digits in decimal numerical system.
