Puzzle 698 Others as 129

From the entry 1156 of the always interesting site of Claudio Meller, we generate the following extension:

Given a zero free number N composed by D distinct digits, obtain all of his D! permutations. Calculate the sum S of all of the D!(D!-1)/2 absolute differences between each two permutations, and report N only if N divides S.

Q1. Obtain the solutions for D=7, 8 & 9. (BTW, confirm that there are NO solutions for D=2 & 5).

Q2. Is there a N prime solution or this is impossible?
