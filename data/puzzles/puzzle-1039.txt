Puzzle 1039. k-number of prime divisors obtained by substitution of...

Metin Sariyar sent the following nice puzzle:

1080787909 is the smallest prime without digits k={2,3,4,5,6} that contains only one 1 such that after replacing 1 with any k (2,3,4,5,6) the new number has k number of prime divisors (multiplicity).

For ex: 61 is prime, 62=2*31 (2 prime divisors) , 63=3*3*7 (3 prime divisors)

1080787909 is prime and 2080787909 = 409 × 5087501,3080787909 = 3 × 71 × 14463793, 4080787909 = 11 × 19 × 757 × 25793, 5080787909 = 172 × 79 × 139 × 1601, 6080787909 = 33 × 7 × 23 × 1398847

Sequence of such numbers I found is: 61(up to 2 ), 61(up to 3), 661(u to 4), 679919 (..5), 1080787909 (..6)

Q. Can you find the next terms smallest prime without digits k={2,3,4,5,6,7} ... {2,3,4,5,6,7,8}... (with multiplicity or distinct prime divisors and squarefree versions )

During the week 16-21 May, 2021, contributions came from Giorgos Kalogeropoulos, Oscar Volpatti, Paul Cleary, Simon Cavegn and Emmanuel Vanieghem.
