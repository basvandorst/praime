Puzzle 730. Trio of Consecutive Achilles numbers

An Achilles number is a number which is powerful but not a perfect power, like 72 = 2^3 * 3^2

According to G. Resta page, there are infinite pairs of consecutive Achilles numbers, the smallest being 5425069447 = 7^3 * 41^2 * 97^2, 5425069448 = 2^3 * 26041^2.

Q. Can you find the smallest trio of consecutive Achilles numbers or prove that they do not exist?
