Puzzle 579.- A triangular array with primes

Jean Brette sent the following nice puzzle:

A triangle is divided in n^2 small triangles, and colored like a chessboard. (here n = 4)

Fill it with distinct primes with the following rule : A prime in a white triangle is equal to the sum of his three neighbors, in the grey triangles. Examples (S is the sum of the primes used)

Q1. Could you find similar triangles for n > 5 ? (with the smallest S, if possible)

Q2. Does it exist a solution for each n ?

Jean Brette himself found one solution using consecutive primes! here is it:

n=3, S=127, Consecutive primes

17

7 29 5

13 23 3 19 11

Q3. Does it exist solutions with consecutive primes for n>3?

Q4. Does it exist solutions using consecutive integers?

Contributions came from Jean Brette (BTW, all of his solutions came without using a PC), Hakan Summakoğlu & Robert D. Mohr.
