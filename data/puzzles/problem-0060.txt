Problem 60. Symmetric primes on each side.

Natalia Makarova share with us a puzzle posted by her here as a distributed computing project:

Find Smallest number such that there are n symmetric (in the gap sense) primes on each side. A055381

Current record (Feb. 10, 2015) is 633925574060895 for which n=22:

4, 9, 12, 30, 30, 165, 8021811, 1071065190, 1613902650, 1797595815015, 633925574060895
