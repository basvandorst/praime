Puzzle 714 Composites that remain composites when...

The number 200 is the minimum number that changing a single digit remains composite (See http://oeis.org/A118118 and also Puzzle 679).

Q. Find the minimum odd composite number not ending in 5 that remains composite changing at a time any two digits by any of the 10x10 combinations of integers 0 to 9, or show that it does not exists.
