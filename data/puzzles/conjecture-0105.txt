Conjecture 105. CONJECTURE ABOUT TWIN PRIMES AND PSEUDOTWIN PRIMES to BASE 3

On May 3, 2024, Davide Rotondo sent the following conjecture:

TEST FOR TWIN PRIMES AND PSEUDOTWIN PRIMES to BASE 3

FOR n>3,

A PAIR OF NUMBERS (n,n+2) PASS THE TEST IF

(3(3^(n-1)-1)+n*(floor((n-2)/3))) is a multiple of n*(n+2).

Examples:

1) 89 & 91:
(3(3^(89-1)-1)+89*(floor((89-2)/3)))/(89*91) =

359219803600761922290463739534787312839

2) 3 &5:
(3(3^(5-1)-1)+5*(floor((5-2)/3)))/(5*7) = 7

Q. true or false?

From Jun 8 to 14, 2024, contributions came from Alessandro Casini

***

Alessandro wrote:

I'm sorry but I can't grasp the content of the conjecture. The test is ok, but what is hypothesized?

Not that all pairs of numbers that pass the test are twin primes, because n = 89 itself would be a counterexample.
