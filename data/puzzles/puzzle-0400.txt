Puzzle 400. 39883

Perhaps you already know the following curio:

The smallest prime which can be represented as sum of a prime and its reversal in five different ways. [Gupta]

As a matter of fact Gupta published the earliest prime of that type in k different ways for k = 1, 2, 3, 4 & 5:

383, 787, 1231, 36263 & 39883

The set of 5 primes associated to 39883by the definition are: 20981 22961 25931 26921 28901, that is to say:

39883=20981+18902=22961+19622=25931+13951=26921+12962=28901+10982

Due that I have not found published the solutions for k>5 I have computed them (again?).

Here are them, for k=6, 7, 8, 9, 10 11, 12 13 & 14:

77267, 77267, 77267, 79687, 80897, 97879, 97879, 118691 & 119701.

The set of primes 14 associated to 119701 are: 20399 23369 24359 25349 26339 27329 28319 41387 44357 46337 49307 82373 87323 & 89303

Questions. Can you extend this sequence for k>14?

Contributions came from J. K. Andersen, Anton Vrba, Farideh Firoozbakht, J. C. Rosa. All extended my initial list by far: Andersen (k=126), Anton (k=90), Farideh (k=31) & Rosa (k=61).
