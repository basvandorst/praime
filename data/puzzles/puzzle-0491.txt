Puzzle 491. Sum of any combination is not prime

Anton Vrba sent the following puzzle:

Add terms to the series 4, 24, 24, 122, 332, 2487, 11847, 40196, 73915, ...

4 is the first composite number

24, 25 are the least two consecutive composite numbers whose sum is not prime

24, 25 and 26 are the least three consecutive consecutive numbers whose sum of any combination is not prime (24+25, 25+26, 24+26 and 24+25+26)

122, 123, 124 and 125 are the least four consecutive composite ......
