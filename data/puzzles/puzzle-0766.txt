Puzzle 766. A Follow up to Puzzle 764.

Dmitry Kamenetsky propose the following nice follow-up to the Puzzle 764

"There is an interesting 2D variant of this problem. Place numbers in a NxN grid such that all gcds from 1 to 2*N*(N-1) are generated. A gcd is generated for every pair of neighboring numbers"

In this problem even small N are hard to solve for the minimal sum.

To give you an example, here is the best result I found for N=3, Sum=251, gcds in red.

10 | 44 | 11 | 2 | 11

40 | 24 | 36 | 8 | 12

35 | 42 | 9 | 7 | 3

10 | 4 | 1 | 251

5 | 6 | 9

Q. Send your minimal sum solutions for N=3, 4 & 5.
