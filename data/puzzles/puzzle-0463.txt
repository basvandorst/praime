Puzzle 463. k.P(k)+(k+1).P(k+1)

JM Bergot sent the following nice puzzle:

It is noticed that

6*P(6) + 7*P(7)=6*13 + 7*17 = 197 prime

7*P(7) + 8*P(8)= 7*17 + 8*19 = 271 prime

8*P(8) + 9*P(9)=8*19 + 9*23 = 359 prime

9*P(9) + 10*P(10)= 9*23 + 10*29 =497 FAIL

There were three successful steps using four consecutive primes from P(6) to P(9).

Can you find a starting prime to give five steps...or more?

Note: The example given by Bergot can be compressed using two numbers: 3 & P(6); the successful steps & the smaller prime used.

Well, I have gotten a solution: 7, P(321150). So the real question is to get a larger than 7 successful steps

Contributions came from Enoch Haga and Farideh Firoozbakht.
