Puzzle 1174 Twin Prime sums between Twin Primes
Paul Cleary sent the following nice puzzle:

Twin Prime sums between Twin Primes.

5 + 7 = 12 is between 11 and 13. There are many examples like this.

211049 + 211051 = 422100 is between 422099 and 422101.

422099 + 422101 = 844200 is between 844199 and 844201.

253679 + 253681 = 507360 is between 507359 and 507361.

507359 + 507361 = 1014720 is between 1014719 and 1014721.

1014719 + 1014721 = 2029440 is between 2029439 and 2029441.

41887255409 + 41887255411 = 83774510820 is between 83774510819 and 83774510821.

83774510819 + 83774510821 = 167549021640 is between 167549021639 and 167549021641.

167549021639 + 167549021641 = 335098043280 is between 335098043279 and 335098043281.

335098043279 + 335098043281 = 670196086560 is between 670196086559 and 670196086561.

Q1. Find the next chain of 4.

Q2. Find and example with a chain of 5 or 6
