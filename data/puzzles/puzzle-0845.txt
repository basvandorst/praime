Puzzle 845. 31 consecutive integers with an even quantity of prime factors

G. L. Honaker, Jr. in his Curio 31, wrote that:

"First occurrence of a run of exactly 31 consecutive integers with an even number of prime factors has never been found."

In the sequence A275508 we may see the first 30 first occurrences:
A275508 First occurrence of a run of exactly n consecutive integers with an even number of prime factors.
1, 9, 14, 33, 54, 201, 140, 2170, 213, 4529, 10403, 1934, 35811, 162144, 38405, 414225, 200938, 389409, 1792209, 5606457, 18493931, 30947532, 61190563, 8405437, 203631499, 577699919, 158280474, 68780189, 4082994208, 3944563444

As a matter of fact other larger occurrences are known. See:
A066793 The start of a record-breaking run of consecutive integers with an even number of prime factors
1, 9, 14, 33, 54, 140, 213, 1934, 35811, 38405, 200938, 389409, 1792209, 5606457, 8405437, 68780189, 880346227, 85910903650, 87635944146, 284340107954, 2195312273836, 2864568747364,
3155591650029.
I have calculated that:
880346227 is a run of 33 integers

85910903650 is a run of 34 integers

87635944146 is a run of 35 integers

284340107954 is a run of 37 integers

2195312273836 is a run of 38 integers

2864568747364 is a run of 41 integers

3155591650029 is a run of 44 integers
Accordingly with this, the sequence A275508 might be written this way, if question marks were admitted:
1, 9, 14, 33, 54, 201, 140, 2170, 213, 4529, 10403, 1934, 35811, 162144, 38405, 414225, 200938, 389409, 1792209, 5606457, 18493931, 30947532, 61190563, 8405437, 203631499, 577699919, 158280474, 68780189, 4082994208, 3944563444, ?, ?, 880346227, 85910903650, 87635944146, ?, 284340107954, 2195312273836, ?, ?, 2864568747364, ?, ?, 3155591650029, ...
Q. Send your integers for exactly 31, 32, 36 39, 40, 42 or 43 consecutive integers with an even quantity of prime factors, or simply another record-breaking run larger than 44.
