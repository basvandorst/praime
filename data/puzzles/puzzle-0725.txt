Puzzle 725. The ABA numbers

Here you are asked just to extend the OEIS sequence A235368 beyond the number 344373768 in order to produce the smallest ABA number with 4 distinct representations.
Q. Send your next terms for this sequence.

Emmanuel wrote:

I can say no more than that the number (2^75)*(3^40)*(5^96) is the smallest ABA number of the form (2^a)(p^b)(q^c). This is a number of 109 digits.

(2^75)*(3^40)*(5^96) = 2*((2^37)*(3^20)*(5^48))^2

= 8*((2^9)*(3^5)*(5^12))^8

= 3*((2^25)*(3^13)*(5^32))^3

= 5*((2^15)*(3^8)*(5^19))^5
