Puzzle 522. Three sets of k consecutive primes

JM Bergot poses the following nice puzzle:

Take three sets of four consecutive primes {2, 3, 5, 7}, {17, 19, 23, 29}, and {127, 131, 137, 139} with no two the same, to get 2|(17+127); 3|(19+131), 5|(23+137) and 7|(29+139).

Q. Can you find three sets with more than four consecutive primes, showing the same property?
