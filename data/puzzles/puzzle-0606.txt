Puzzle 606. Puzzle 738 from Claudio Meller's site

Claudio Meller in his interesting site, published the Puzzle 738.

Find all the integers composed only by prime-digits such that its square remain composed only by prime-digits.

Claudio states that apparently the only integers having such property are: 5, 235 & 72335.

Q1. Can verify the Claudio's statement or find a counterexample?

Q2. Redo Q1 admitting "1" as prime-digit.

Contributions came from Torbjörn Alm, Emmanuel Vantieghem & Hakan Summakoğlu
