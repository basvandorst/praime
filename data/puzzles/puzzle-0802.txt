Puzzle 802. A follow up to Puzzle 801

Now please use the following schemes following scheme:

Scheme #1

p*np(p)+2=q

q*np(q)+2=r

and so on...

where np(x) is the next larger prime to x.

and the following scheme:

Scheme #2

p*np(p)-2=q

q*np(q)-2=r

and so on...

Q. Find the prime p generating the largest stepladder you may produce using both schemes.
