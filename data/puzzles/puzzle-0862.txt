Puzzle 862. Redo Puzzle 861

Redo Puzzle 861, including the arithmetic multiplication operation to the two previous accepted operations (adding and subtracting).

Example: For k=3, N={2, 3, 7}, n=9

2=2

3=3

5=2+3

7=7

11=2*7-3

13= 2*3+7

17=2*7+3

19=3*7-2

23=3*7+2

Q=n/k=9/3=3.

I'm not sure if this example is optimum for k=3.

Q. Send your solution with your best Q value for k=2, 3, 4, ... 10.

Contributions came from Dmitry Kamenetsky, Michael Hürter and Emmanuel Vantieghem
