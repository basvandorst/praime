Puzzle 399. Some more terms

Farideh asks:

Maybe some of your puzzlers can find some more terms of the following three nice sequences:

A090872, A090874 & A090875.

Contributions came from J. K. Andersen & Giovanni Resta:

***

Andersen wrote (6/5/07):

A090872(6) is 2072005925466, since these are primes:

2072005925466^1+1

2072005925466^2+1

2072005925466^4+1

2072005925466^8+1

2072005925466^16+1

2072005925466^32+1

2072005925466^64+1

The solution can also be added to puzzle 137. Yves Gallot's estimate of 10^14 in puzzle 137 was too high.

***

Giovanni wrote (11/5/07)

the next term of A090872 = a(n), is the smallest number m greater than 1 such that m^2^k+1 for k=0,1,...,n are primes is 2072005925466. I stopped my search there.

I've searched also for A090875 = a(n) is the smallest number m such that m^(2^k) + 2^(2^k) is prime for k=0,1,...,n. up to 2,365,100,000,000 but I did not find the next term.
