Puzzle 1096 37 & 73 coming from 10

JM Bergot sent the following nice puzzle:
For composite 10, the sum from 1 to 10 is

55, and the sum of the divisors of 10 is 18,

giving 55-18=37 and 55+18=73. 37 & 73 are emirps.
Q. Can you find other emirps from a composite larger than 10?

On July 30, 2022 Michael Branicky wrote:

None < 373000000 as of now.

Later he wote: I kept searching and found no other such composite <= 10^9.

Stopping there.
