Puzzle 1215. Find more primes like 23497

The following nice puzzle was sent by Emmanuel Vantieghem

23497 is the smallest distinct-digit prime which is the sum of powers of its digits in four different ways :

23497 = 2^5 + 3^4 + 4^2 + 9^4 + 7^5

23497 = 2^5 + 3^8 + 4^2 + 9^2 + 7^5

23497 = 2^7 + 3^4 + 4^7 + 9^4 + 7^3

23497 = 2^7 + 3^8 + 4^7 + 9^2 + 7^3.

Q1. Find three more primes like 23497.

Q2.Find the greatest distinct-digits-prime with four representations as sum of powers of its digits

Q3. Find the smallest distinct-digits-prime with more than four representations (k>4) of sum of powers of its digits.

Q4. Since the number of distinct-digit primes is finite, there must be a greatest k for which there exists at least one distinct-digit prime with k representations. Send your example with the largest k you can find.
