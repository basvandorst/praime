Puzzle 1240 09/27/2025

Past Saturday, 09/27/2025, was a very special day because this day, written in the USA date-format (mm/dd/yyyy) and rewritten in the Europe & América Latina date-format (dd/mm/yyyy), 27/09/2025 provides integers that are perfect squares:

Usa Format: 09272025 = 3045^2

Europe & América Latina format: 27092025 = 5205^2

Q1. Are other dates with the same property?

Q2. Are there dates in any (if both, the better!) of the two formats, that are prefect prime squares (0001<yyyy<9999)?

Note: Please never discard leading zeros.

From Oct 4 to 10, 2025, contributions came from Michael Branicky, Giorgos Kalogeropoulos, Jeff Heleen, Gennady Gusev, Emmanuel Vantieghem,
