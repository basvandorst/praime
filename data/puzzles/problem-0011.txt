Problem 11.- Here you are asked to find primes of the following form

(p^r - 1)/(p^d -1), expressible in more than one way.

Batman asks if 31=(2^5 - 1)/(2 -1)=(5^3 -1)/(5-1) is the only primes which is expressible in more than one way in the form (p^r - 1)/(p^d -1) where p is prime also, r=>3, and d=>1. No other has been found below 10^10.

If the condition that p be prime is relaxed the problem goes to Goormaghtigh and we have the only known solution :

8191=(2^13-1)/(21)=(90^31)/(90-1).

In both cases : can you find other examples?

(Ref. 2, p. 81, B25)

(See also Puzzle 61)
