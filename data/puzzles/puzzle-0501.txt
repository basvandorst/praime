Puzzle 501. Three Consecutive primes a prime

JM Bergot sent the following puzzle related to three consecutive primes:

7+(11+13)=31

31+(37+41)=109

109+(113+127)=349

349+(353+359)=1061

1061+(1063+1069)=3193 (Fail)

Q. Can you find a larger (than 4) successful steps like these above?

My comment: This puzzle had been posed before (See Puzzle 421). That time JKA reached to one solution length 10
