Problems & Puzzles: Puzzles

Puzzle 659 Sandwiched palindromes

Here we ask for palindrome integers P such that P+1 & P-1 are prime integers.
The smallest example is 282.

Q. Send your largest &/or nice P case.

Contributions came from Torbjörn Alm, W. Edwin Clark, Giovanni Resta, Emmanuel Vantieghem, Hakan Summakoglu, Farideh Firoozbakht & Igor Schein.
