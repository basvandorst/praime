Puzzle 445. Consecutive integers with prime SPF

JM Bergot sent the following nice question:

Take 197,198,199. The factors extended for

198=2*3*3*11 with sum 19. Since both 197 and 199

are prime, these three consecutive numbers each have

the sum of their prime factors being a prime.

How many consecutive numbers can you find with this

property?

Carlos Rivera found an example of 10 consecutive numbers with this property, starting on 896531141.

Questions: Can you get larger rows of these?

J. K. Andersen wrote (May 08):

The first case of 11 numbers starts at 183208285259.
