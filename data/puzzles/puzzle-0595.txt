Puzzle 595. p*q -> p^2 + p*q + q^2

JM Bergot sent the following nice puzzle:

From a semiprime p*q get p^2 + p*q + q^2...

Let's tart with the semiprime 10=2*5

2*5--->2^2 + 10 + 5^2=39=3*13

3*13--->3^2 + 39 + 13^2=217=7*31

7*31--->49+217+961=1227=3*409

3*409--->9+1227+167,281=168,517=43*3919

43*3919--->43^2 + 168517 + 3919^2=15,528,927=3*5,176,309

...

Q1. How far goes the semiprime 10?

Q2. Can you get a larger chain?

Contributions came from Jim Howell, Torbjörn Alm, Antoine Verroken & Hakan Summakoğlu

***

All of them discovered that starting with the semiprime 10 you can go one more step:

3*5176309--->3^2 + 15528927 + 5176309^2=26794190392417=7*3827741484631

7*3827741484631--->7^2 + 26794190392417 + 3827741484631^2=14651604873191926199598627 = 3 * 67 * 211 * 345467092810637009257 (Not a semiprime).
