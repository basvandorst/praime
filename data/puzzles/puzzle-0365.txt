Puzzle 365. P*Q = R*S

One more puzzle related to pandigitals before switching to other issues:

Let P, Q, R & S to be zero-less pandigitals, such that:

P*Q=R*S=Z

Example: 234579618 * 234589176 = 351869427 * 156392784

Questions.

1. Find the minimal & maximal Z solution

2. Find the minimal & maximal Z solution if Z is "twice zero-less pandigital"

3. Redo 1 & 2 for pandigitals.

Contributions came from Farideh Firoozbakht.
