I found that prime 41 summed with 8 consecutive primorials gives 8 primes:

1) 41 + 2# = 43 - prime

2) 41 + 3# = 47 - prime

3) 41 + 5# = 71 - prime

4) 41 + 7# = 251 - prime

7) 41 + 17# = 510551 - prime

Q1. Find a longer sequence (not necessarily starting from 2#).
