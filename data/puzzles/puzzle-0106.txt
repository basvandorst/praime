Puzzle 106. Prime-magic squares added up to 2000

John Everett sent to me (last July 24, 2000) his third letter crowded of interesting and beauty Prime-Magical Squares solutions to different situations. I have selected only one of them before the current year ends.

(A_1 + A_2 = 2000)_4x4

67 | 241 | 577 | 571 | | 1933 | 1759 | 1423 | 1429 | | 2000 | 2000 | 2000 | 2000

547 | 769 | 127 | 13 | | 1453 | 1231 | 1873 | 1987 | | 2000 | 2000 | 2000 | 2000

223 | 139 | 421 | 673 | + | 1777 | 1861 | 1579 | 1327 | = | 2000 | 2000 | 2000 | 2000

619 | 307 | 331 | 199 | | 1381 | 1693 | 1669 | 1801 | | 2000 | 2000 | 2000 | 2000

Naively I tried to obtain one corresponding example for (A_1 + A_2 = 2000)_3x3 but for my surprise - and as far as my method works - it doesn't exists!... Nevertheless 2000 is not inaccessible from the 3x3 Prime-Magical squares...

Questions:

Regarding the Prime Magic "equation" (A_1 + A_2 + ... + A_k = 2000)_3x3 1. Find one minimal-k solution

2. Find one maximal-k-solution

3. Maybe it's time enough to start producing one solution to (A_1 + A_2 + A_3 = 2001)_3x3 - if any exist...
