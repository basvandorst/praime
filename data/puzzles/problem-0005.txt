Problem 5.- Missed prime factors in Woodall numbers

A Woodall number is of the following form : n*2^n -1

The smallest Woodall numbers with unknown prime factors are :

368*2^368 -1 = 29*3233254457712259*C97

388*2^388 -1= 3*57389*21883688258780797*C98

395*2^395 -1= 3*11*587*8467*648624223293259*C99

409*2^409 -1 = 11*311*541*49333*5999187334683223*C99

would you like to try to search for those unknown prime factors hidden

in the composites C97, C98, & C99’s ?

***

388*2^388 -1 & 409*2^409 -1 have been already completely factorised (kindly advised to this pages by Jim Howell, 17/oct/98 & 29/Jan/99). Howell also suggest to add to this problem the following Woodall numbers with unknown prime factors:

W380 has factor C100

W441 has factor C100

W542 has factor C100 (factorised by Jo Yeong Uk, see below)
