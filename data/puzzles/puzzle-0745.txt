Puzzle 745. A number 20 digits large, best qualified

Here we ask you to get a 20 digits integer N having two digits of each from 0 to 9 such that for each prime string of large L inside it you are able to add L to the qualification of N.

Q. Get your best qualified N integer

a) Reading the primes only from left to right.

b) Reading the primes in both directions.

Please send only you best qualified integer N for each question and the corresponding primes.
