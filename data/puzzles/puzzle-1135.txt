Puzzle 1135 Polynomial with consecutive primes such that...

Davide Rotondo sent the following puzzle:
The polynomial n^4 + 29n^2 + 101 produces 19 consecutive primes whose digits do not alternate parity, for n=1 to 19

The polynomial of Kazmenko and Trofimov

-66n^3 + 3845n^2 - 60897n + 251831

It produces 46 consecutive primes which I noticed to be peculiar in that for n ranging from 0 to 21 and from 23 to 45 the prime numbers do not have alternating parity digits

Q. ARE THERE OTHER POLYNOMIAL THAT PRODUCE MORE CONSECUTIVE PRIMES

WITH NO ALTERNATE PARITY DIGITS?
