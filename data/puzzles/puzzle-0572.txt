Puzzle 572.- (P+Q)@3<>0

JM Bergot sent the following puzzle:

Look for consecutive primes such that added every two contiguous the sum is not divided by 3

I [CR] got 21 of such sums with the following 22 consecutive primes & the minimal for this run:

766319189 766319201 766319231 766319237 766319249 766319261 766319273 766319291 766319339 766319357 766319363 766319369 766319423 766319441 766319453 766319483 766319507 766319549 766319573 766319579 766319621 766319627

Q. Can you find a largest run of these type of consecutive primes (if the minimal for the run, the better)
