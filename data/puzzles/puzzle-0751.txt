Puzzle 751. Named primes producing primes.

Here you are asked to select ten letters such that you can use them to name in your local language as many primes as you can.

Then you must assign to each one of the ten letters one of the integers from 0 to 9 in such a way that the numerical interpretation of each named prime produces a prime number too.

Example:

In Spanish, I have produced 7 primes with the following letter-selection and integer assignations to these letters:

"DIECNUVSTR"-->"7513248906"

TRES (3)=0619

SIETE(7)=95101

TRECE(13)=06131

DIECISIETE(17)=7513595101

DIECINUEVE(19)=7513524181

VEINTITRES(23)=8152050619

VEINTINUEVE(29)=81520524181

Q. Make your own letters selection and integer assignation to these letters to produce K primes in your local language associated to the K named primes, for K>7 as large as you can.
