Puzzle 788. P&p(i)^2&RP

Vicente F. Izquierdo sent the following nice puzzle:
Find the smallest prime P such that the first k equations are simultaneously prime numbers.

P&2^2&RP

P&3^2&RP

P&5^2&RP

...

P&p(k)^2&RP

Where RP is the reversible of P. The symbol & means concatenation.

Vicente found the minimal P for k=6:

123638027&{2,3,5,7,11,13}^2&720836321 are the following prime numbers:
1236380274720836321

1236380279720836321

12363802725720836321

12363802749720836321

123638027121720836321

123638027169720836321
As a mater of fact he found the minimal P for k=1 to 6:

11,17,1097,7949,780587, 123638027,...

Q1. Find more terms

Q2. Show that this sequence is finite or infinite.

Contributions came from Vicente F. Izquierdo, Abhiram R. Devesh and Emmanuel Vantieghem. They found the next term on the sequence for Q1. Nobody posted anything about Q2.
