Puzzle 927. Prime solutions to HTP

For sure you already know the classical "Hexagonal Tortoise Problem". If not, please take a look to the details here.

It's already known that if you fill the 30 vertices with the integers 1-30, no repetition allowed, there are several solutions with magical constants that vary from 77 to 109.

Here is the solution with magic constant = 93

Here (on pages 9-10) you can see all the solutions from 77 to 93.

Q1. What are the solutions from 94 to 109?

Q2. If possible, find the minimal (magic constant) solutions using 30 distinct primes a) consecutive, b) non-consecutive?

Contributions came from Seiji Tomita.
