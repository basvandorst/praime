Puzzle 19.- Primes on a clock

How many primes are in a clock if you can read the numbers around the circle clockwise and counterclockwise (the primes should have no more than 15 digits) ?

How many prime numbers are in a digital clock ?

Solution part 2.

Tiziano Mosconi has solved (13/8/2001) the question 2:

Primes Clockwise:
{2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 23, 67, 89, 101, 4567, 10111, 67891, 89101, 789101, 4567891, 23456789, 56789101, 1234567891, 45678910111, 12345678910111}

They are twenty.

Primes Clockwise:
{2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 19, 43, 101, 211, 1019, 1987, 2111, 21211, 76543, 101987, 432121, 4321211, 12111019, 6543212111, 9876543212111}

They are twenty.

As you can see, there are some primes that appear both in the clockwise and in the counterclockwise case, so the total number of primes that appear in a clock is less than 20+20... They are 34
