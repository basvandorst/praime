Puzzle 953. Q2 from Puzzle 658.

Carlos Rivera posed this puzzle:

Redo Q2, Puzzle 658 for your local language:

“What is the smallest spelled-out prime number that has one of one letter, two of another letter, three of another letter, ..., and ten of another letter?”

The Spanish solution already solved by Carlos Rivera was this one:

1326605911, "mil trescientos veintiseis millones seiscientos cinco mil, novecientos once", where r=1, v=2, m=3, l=4, t=5, c=6, o=7, n=8, s=9, e=10, i=11
