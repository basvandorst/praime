Round 60, written before the entropy of the twin gaps is computed (the counts themselves were taken in round 59).

Entropy of the distance d between consecutive twin primes (own derivation). Leading-order law on d in 6N:
P(d) proportional to S({0,2,d,d+2})/S({0,2}) e^(-d rho_tw), rho_tw = 2 C2/lam^2. The local factor at p >= 5 is
proportional to (1 - nu/p) with nu = 2 (d = 0 mod p), 3 (d = +-2), 4 (otherwise), and its mean over d is
p(p-2)/(p-1)^2; at p = 2, 3 it is constant on 6N. So
  H(twin gap) = log2(e/(6 rho_tw)) - K_tw/ln 2,  K_tw = sum_{p>=5} (1/p) sum_d w_d ln w_d,  w = local factor / its mean,
a closed sum over primes (computed in r60.py before the comparison).
Data: the 2,963,534 twin gaps of [1e9, 2e9) (round 59), plug-in entropy with Miller-Madow. Also the entropy of round
59's conditioned law itself (averaged over the window) and of the discrete leading-order law.
Reading fixed now: the data entropy agrees with round 59's law to within 0.01 bits, and the asymptote lies within
0.1 bits of the data (the approach is expected to be slow, as for the prime gaps, rounds 52 and 57).
