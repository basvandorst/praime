Why. Round 143 confirmed the swapped term between two Dirichlet L-functions (slope 1.0002 +- 0.0031); the review showed
that its shape comes from "lines" (products of prime-number-theorem-in-progressions means, theorem) and that the
conjecture is a zero-mean continuum. Round 144 put 138 elliptic curves' zeros on disk. In degree 2 a prime-side
argument suggested that the one-point sums have no main term (a_p is not a function of a residue class), so that the
cross term would vanish. The derivation (derivation.md sections 2-4) shows instead:
 - the one-point sum is a prime sum with the GL(2) Voronoi kernel e(-2 sqrt(n/N)), which resonates at dual index 1;
   the recipe gives sum_g e^{2i theta_E(g)} = -w c_E (T2 - T1)/2pi, c_E = B_E/L(1, Sym^2 E), and lines at every
   xi = ln(a/b) with amplitude -w c_E rho(a/b)/sqrt(ab) (recipe-level; a prime number theorem for the resonance, not a
   theorem). Measured (not blind): slope 0.989 +- 0.011 on all 158 curves, 0.9875 +- 0.0116 on the 138 fresh curves.
 - the recipe's double-swap term is exactly the sum over rational detunings of the products of the two curves' lines
   (proved from the recipe), = (1/4 pi^2) w1 w2 c1 c2 int |L(1 + iu, E1 x E2)|^2 A12(iu) K(u) du.
 So the scout's "no term" hypothesis is refuted already by the one-point sums; what remains to test blind is (i) the
lines at xi != 0 on fresh curves, (ii) the H - 1 part of the cross term, (iii) the zero-mean continuum (independence
of the fluctuations of two different curves: shifted convolution sums of two Hecke eigenvalue sequences over primes).

The law (recipe; derivation.md section 3). E1, E2 semistable, non-isogenous, conductors N_j, root numbers w_j,
theta_j(t) = (t/2) ln N_j - t ln 2 pi + Im lnGamma(1 + it), K(u) = exp(-u^2/2 sigma^2):
   X12 = (1/T) sum_{g1, g2} e^{2i theta1(g1) - 2i theta2(g2)} K(g2 - g1) w((g1+g2)/2)   (window W = [20, 396], T = 376),
   E X12 = (1/4 pi^2) w1 w2 c1 c2 J12,  J12 = int H12(u) K(u) du,
   H12(u) = prod_p sum_v rho1(p^v) rho2(p^v) p^{-|v|} p^{iuv} = |L(1 + iu, E1 x E2)|^2 A12(iu),
   c_E = B_E/L(1, Sym^2 E), rho(p^v) = p^{|v|/2} S_p(v)/F_p(1/p), S_p(v) = sum_{m-h=v} lambda(p^m) mu_E(p^h) p^{-(m+h)/2}.
 J from exact local factors for p <= 3000 and all bad primes, first-order lines (+ the constant second-order term) to
 1e5; numerical error below 1e-4 in units of the prediction (checks in s5_check_out.txt; independent recomputation in
 validate_out.txt).
One-point law and lines (recipe): G(ln r) = sum_{20 < g <= 400} e^{2i theta(g)} r^{-ig} = -w c rho(r)/sqrt(ab) * 380/2pi
 + o(T), r = a/b in {1, 2, 1/2, 3, 1/3, 4, 1/4, 5, 1/5, 7, 1/7, 3/2, 2/3}; Hann taper: half.
