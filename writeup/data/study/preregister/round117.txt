How large should the rigidity drop of round 116 be? Berry's diagonal approximation: the zeros' density fluctuation is
-(1/pi) sum_{p,k} chi(p^k) ln p p^(-k/2) cos(k t ln p), so prime p adds to the connected pair correlation (unfolded,
density L/2pi, L = ln(q t / 2 pi)) the term (2/L^2) sum_k (ln p)^2 p^(-k) cos(2 pi eps k ln p / L). Removing p changes the
number variance in a window of one mean spacing by
  dSigma2(1) = -(4/L^2) sum_k (ln p)^2 p^(-k) (1 - cos a_k)/a_k^2,   a_k = 2 pi k ln p / L      (ours, no parameter).
