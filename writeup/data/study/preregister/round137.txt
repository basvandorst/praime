THE LAW (Hardy-Littlewood; lam = ln x, C = phi(q), N = H/lam)
  Exact (proved): E[within]/(C-1)^2 = 1 + (2/(C-1)^2) sum_j (1 - j/N) tau_j, where tau_j is the lag-j covariance of
  the within-projected transition increments. tau_1 is a linear functional of the second-order Markov deviation
  Delta(a,b,c) = D3(a,b,c) - D2(a,b) P(c|b) of three consecutive primes.
  Leading order (Lemke Oliver-Soundararajan's non-adjacent term, conjecture):
    k = 1: 1 - 1/((C-1) lam);    k = 2: 1 - 1/(3(C-1) lam) + 2 B_q^2/(C-1)^2,  B_q = C P(p_{n+1} = p_n mod q) - 1.
  Finite lam (used for every prediction below; no fitted parameter), each term from the Hardy-Littlewood conditioned
  expansion at y = 13:
    - tau_1 from the triple law;
    - tau_j (j >= 2) from the chain on the rough classes mod 30030;
    - for k = 2, tau_2 from the leading LOS term -1/(6(C-1)lam);
    - a long-range tail from the HL pair correlations of the primes > 13 acting on the insertion responses of the
      table, of slope -V/((C-1)^2 lam) per unit ln H.
  Law error per cell: truncation (y = 13 against 11), the pair weight (w2 against w3), +-40 % of the tail, and 0.002
  for the k = 2 lag-2 term.

STATISTIC (exactly writeup/code/study/r135b.py, run as `r135b.py new <24 x0 values>`)
- Tables (q,k) = (3,1), (3,2), (5,1), (7,1), (10,2); block lengths 1e4, 1e5, 1e6.
- Per window, the mean over blocks of within/(C-1)^2 * NB/(NB-1).
- Pooled value: the mean over the 24 windows.
- Two errors: sig_block = sqrt(sum of the 24 per-window block errors^2)/24, and sig_scatter = std(24 window
  means)/sqrt(24). sig_meas = max(sig_block, sig_scatter). sig_tot = sqrt(sig_meas^2 + law_err^2).
- (10,2) is the same table as (5,2) for odd primes; the law was computed as (5,2).

Verdict rule: the law is confirmed if R1, R2, R3 and R4 pass. The sieve chain is rejected if R1 rejects it in 2 of 3
cells, R3 rejects "no tail", and R4's (3,2) rejects it. A pass of R1 and R4 with R2 failing (T < 0.40) leaves the
height dependence open. A pass of R2 with R1 failing in one cell is reported as a partial confirmation, with the cell
named.

Known weak points, stated now:
- The law's error is dominated by the +-40 % on the long-range tail, which comes from the validation stretch. That
  limits R1 and R3.
- The k = 2 lag-2 term is LOS's leading-order value; the chain has no memory beyond one step.
- O(1/N) ratio and edge terms are not modelled (estimated at 1e-3 or less at H = 1e4).
- q = 5 fell about twice as fast with H as the law at the old height (-1.4 sigma at 1e6).
