Why. Round 142 measured the change of Var log|L'(rho)| per prime dividing the conductor (-0.194, -0.107, -0.054, -0.033
for p = 2, 3, 5, 7) and had no theory for it: the leading order -Li2(1/p)/2 is off by 60 se, the registered heuristic
(LDM) by 18 se (amplitude 0.74). Here the variance is written exactly through the zeros (Hadamard product) as a Palm
functional of the two- and three-point correlation functions (checked on CUE(N) to 1e-15), and the registered law of
rounds 134-138 (ratios conjecture / Bogomolny-Keating with the bad primes deleted) is integrated without any model step.

Law (derivation.md):
  V (conjecture-level input = the registered T2, T3; all else proved or computed): for a bad set S at fixed L,
     dVar(S, L) = sum_{p in S}[ -Li2(1/p)/2 + dB_off + dA + dV3_Q + dV3_P ] + (pair terms) - [(mu + dmu(S))^2 - mu^2],
     with the closed forms dV3_Q = (ln p/L) ln^2(p/(p-1)), dmu_diag = ln p ln(p/(p-1))/L,
     dA_diag = -(2/L) sum_m ln p (gamma_E + ln(2 pi m ln p/L))/(m p^m), mu = gamma_E + ln 2pi - 1 + (gamma_E + C1)/L + mu_off,
     and the numerical off-diagonal and three-point pieces. First order (derived): -Li2(1/p)/2 + (ln p/L) l (2 + l),
     l = ln(p/(p-1)) (= the LDM's first-order term; the LDM misses only second and higher orders).
     At fixed L = 10.2: -0.191, -0.104, -0.050, -0.031, -0.017, -0.013 for p = 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13.
Hypotheses for the alternatives: H_LDM (round 142's registered heuristic: leading order + first-order term only);
H_lead (leading order only); H_none (no dependence on the conductor's primes).
