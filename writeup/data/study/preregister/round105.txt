Third face of one number. Round 104: prime-rich quadratics (chi_D(p) = -1 for small p) have large L'/L(1, chi_D) and a low
first zero. The prime race between the primes with chi_D(p) = -1 and those with chi_D(p) = +1 is then biased towards
-1 (Chebyshev bias; Rubinstein-Sarnak: the bias is large when low zeros carry weight). Prediction, for the same 3,040
fundamental discriminants 3 <= |D| <= 5000 (writeup/code/study/lowzero/r104.json):
  bias(D) = share of the primes p <= 10^5 (p not dividing D) at which #{q <= p : chi(q) = -1} > #{q <= p : chi(q) = +1}.
(1) Spearman correlation of bias(D) with the scaled first zero g (round 104) is at most -0.5 for each sign of D;
(2) its correlation with S = 2 L'/L(1) + ln(q/pi) + psi((1+a)/2) is at least +0.5 for each sign;
(3) the median bias of the 100 discriminants with the lowest g exceeds 0.95.
