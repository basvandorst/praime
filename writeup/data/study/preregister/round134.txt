Round 134, pre-registered before the computation (commit order shows the order; STUDY.md section 1). No zero set was

Why. Round 123 found Berry's per-prime diagonal law right to first order over l = 0.5..8, but with a residual of period 1
in l (too negative at whole l, too positive at half l, growing with l; chi2 15-85 on 6) and a failure at l = 0.5 (slope
0.56 real, 0.30 complex). The Bogomolny-Keating two-point function for primitive Dirichlet L-functions (J. Phys. A 46
(2013) 095202, arXiv:1307.6010) has an off-diagonal term (1/4pi^2) e^{i eps L} |zeta(1+i eps)|^2 Phi_off(eps) Psi_off(eps,k)
+ c.c., L = ln(kE/2pi), with Psi_off = prod_{p|k} F_p, F_p = (p - p^{-i eps})/(p - 2 + p^{i eps}). In unfolded units
e^{i eps L} = e^{2 pi i r}. A prime p | k changes the number variance by Berry's diagonal term
  dSigma2_diag(l; p, L) = -(2/pi^2) sum_m sin^2(pi l m ln p/L)/(m^2 p^m)
plus an off-diagonal term
  dSigma2_off(l; p, L, S) = (4/L^2) int_0^l (l - r) Re[e^{2 pi i r} |zeta(1+i eps)|^2 Phi_off(eps) prod_{q in S} F_q(eps)
                            (F_p(eps) - 1)] dr,  eps = 2 pi r/L, S = the other primes dividing k.
The off-diagonal term has period 1 in l and vanishes at whole l to leading order. It is not additive over bad primes.
Derivation, code and checks: writeup/code/study/lowzero/bk/ (derivation.md, bklib.py, predict.py, validate.py; an
independent recomputation of the integrals agrees to
1.5e-8).
