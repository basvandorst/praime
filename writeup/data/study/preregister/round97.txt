The swing of the long-interval prime variance and the zeros' repulsion (ours). Under RH and linear independence of
the gamma's, the off-diagonal part of R(X, d) has mean square over ln X equal to (4/d^2) Q, with
  Q = sum_{j != k} |c_j|^2 |c_k|^2 / (4 + (g_j - g_k)^2),  c = ((1+d)^rho - 1)/rho, both signs of gamma.
Hypothesis: the zeros miss, compared with a smooth (Poisson) density of partners, on average exactly one neighbour
(GUE sum rule, int (sin pi u/pi u)^2 du = 1), so
  Q_actual = Q_smooth - sum_j |c_j|^4 K(L_j),  K(L) = int (sin pi u/pi u)^2 / (4 + (2 pi u/L)^2) du, L_j = ln(|g_j|/2pi),
where Q_smooth uses the actual zeros for j and the smooth density ln(|t|/2pi)/2pi for the partner (FFT convolution).
Computation with the 10,000 zeros (zeros_10000_flint.txt) at d = 0.1 and 0.01.
