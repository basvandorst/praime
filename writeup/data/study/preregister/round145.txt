Round 145, pre-registered: moments of low zeros over all characters mod a prime q beyond the proved Gaussian range, read from primes alone (a measurement round).
modulus, inside and beyond the mock-Gaussian range (Hughes-Rudnick 2003). Committed with predictions_frozen.json
before any character value of the fresh moduli was computed. Development: ONE modulus, q = 10007 (pilot; used).
Derivation and code: derivation.md, mock_sum.py (statistic), mock_test.py (predictions, readings), power.py,
extrap_check.py, t1_power.py, validate_fft.py, validate_zeros.py.

Why. Hughes-Rudnick prove that the m-th moment of the prime part of W_f over the q-2 non-principal characters mod a
prime q is Gaussian when supp f-hat is inside (-2/m, 2/m) and conjecture that beyond it the moments follow U(N)
(non-Gaussian). No numerical test of the region beyond was found. The pilot shows that at q ~ 1e4 the finite-q
structure is O(1) relative to the N = infinity random-matrix values, so a pass/fail test of those values is NOT
registered. Registered instead: (T0) the exact finite-q decomposition in the proven range; (T1) the conjectured
Gaussian extension beyond the proof (kappa_3 up to s = 1, kappa_4 to s = 0.7) against the exact finite-q baseline;
(T3) whether the deviation of the fourth cumulant from U(N) shrinks with q. Everything else: reported, no reading.

Statistic. f-hat_s(u) = (1 - |u|/s)_+. For every non-principal chi mod q: P_s(chi) = W_f(chi) - Wbar_{a(chi)} =
-(2/log q) sum_{n < q^s, q not| n} Lambda(n) n^-1/2 f-hat_s(log n/log q) Re chi(n) (explicit formula, checked against
PARI zeros of all 248 characters mod 101 and 151 to 1.5e-8). One FFT of length q-1 per s (checked against direct
sums, 200 characters, to 2e-14). kappa_m = population cumulants over the family (each character once), m = 2, 3, 4.

the family moments up to order 8 with n_eff = (q-1)/2 conjugate pairs (pilot: bootstrap over pairs agrees within
~10 %). Checked on the data: within each group of 4 moduli, chi^2 of (S_m - group mean)/se over the T0 cells, reported;
if chi^2/dof > 3 the se are rescaled by sqrt(chi^2/dof) for T1/T3 and this is reported.
