REGISTRATION, round 160: the murmuration density of weight-2 newforms at PRIME levels (natural weights).

STATEMENT (derived in writeup/code/study/lowzero/universal/murmur/murmur_gate.md, section 3.4, by averaging the one-point law
with its lines over prime levels; its line amplitudes also follow from fact 2054 with Hecke twists): for N prime,
  E_N[eps_f a_f(p)] = sum_f eps_f a_f(p) / dim  ->  M_prime(y) = 2 pi sqrt(y) - 12 y + (12/pi) sum_{1 <= r <= 2 sqrt y} nu'(r) sqrt(4y - r^2),
  y = p/N, nu'(r) = sum over the squarefree delta | r of c(delta) (densities.py, M_prime). No parameter is fitted.
  Zubrilina's density M_zub (Theorem 1 of arXiv 2310.07681) is for squarefree levels; a footnote there says that densities
  differ between families of levels. The prime-level formula was not found in the sources read so far (a second gate is running).
