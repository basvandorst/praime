L5 for races between any two residue classes. For a prime q, pi(x;q,a) = (1/phi(q)) sum_chi conj(chi(a)) pi(x, chi) and,
from the explicit formula, pi(x, chi) (ln x/sqrt x) ~ -sum_gamma x^(i gamma)/rho - eps(chi), eps = 1 for real chi (the prime
squares), 0 for complex chi. So the race b against 1 is
  E_b(x) = (1/phi(q)) sum_{chi != chi_0} (conj chi(b) - conj chi(1)) (-sum_gamma x^(i gamma)/rho_chi - eps(chi)),
class b ahead where E_b > 0. Predicted from the zeros with |gamma| <= 50 of every non-principal character mod q (PARI), for
q = 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, 29, 31 and every b = 2 .. q-1: the share of primes p <= 1e6 at which class b leads class 1.
Measured from a sieve to 1e6 (ties count as not ahead).
Readings: (1) Pearson correlation of predicted and measured shares over all races >= 0.9; (2) mean absolute error <= 0.08.
