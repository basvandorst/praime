RIEMANN section 23 notes, unexplained, that the unfolded nearest-neighbour spacing variance of the first 10,000 zeros of
zeta is 0.154-0.156 against 0.178-0.180 for GUE. Claim: this is the same arithmetic rigidity as L6 (no prime power below 2,
Berry's saturation), which every L-function shares, and at finite height it depends on the height only through the
density parameter L = ln(q t / 2 pi) (Bogomolny-Bohigas-Leboeuf-Monastra's effective matrix size grows with L).
Data: zeros of zeta (writeup/data/riemann/zeros_10000_flint.txt) and of L(s, chi_D) for D = -3, -4, 5, -163, 1009, -3635
(PARI lfunzeros, about 3,000-6,000 zeros each), unfolded with N(t) = (t/2 pi) ln(q t/(2 pi e)) (+ 7/8 for zeta), spacings
binned by L in bins of width 1.
Readings: (1) in every bin with at least 800 spacings, every L-function's spacing variance lies below the GUE value 0.180
(Gaudin); (2) in each L-bin where zeta and at least one L(s, chi) both have 800 spacings, the values agree within 3 standard
errors (bootstrap over blocks of 50 spacings), i.e. the deficit is a function of L, not of the conductor.
