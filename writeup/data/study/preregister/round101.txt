Fact 2049 for an even primitive character (ours): for even chi, L(0,chi) = 0, so -L'/L(s,chi)/(s(s-1)) has a double
pole at s = 0, giving G_chi(x) = sum_rho x^rho/(rho(1-rho)) + ln x + 1 + B0 - T_e(x), B0 = lim_{s->0} (L'/L(s,chi) - 1/s),
T_e(x) = sum_{n even >= 2} x^(-n)/(n(n+1)); under GRH |G_chi - ln x - 1 - B0 + T_e| <= C sqrt(x), equality at x = 1,
C = L'/L(1,chi) - B0 - ln 2 (from x = 1) = 2 Re L'/L(1,chi) + ln(q/pi) + psi(1/2) (Hadamard).
Character: the Legendre symbol mod 5 (even, real). Primes and prime powers up to 1e9.
Readings: (1) the two expressions for C agree (an identity by the functional equation; checks the derivation of the
s = 0 term); (2) the maximum of |G - ln x - 1 - B0 + T_e|/sqrt(x) on [100, 1e9] is at most C; (3) heuristic: it is
between 0.3 and 0.8 of C.
