THE LAW (general_law.md of round 1; recipe-level for degree >= 2, a theorem for degree 1). L primitive, analytic
normalisation, conductor Q, gamma shifts mu_j, root number eps (PARI lfunrootres), theta_L(t) = (t/2) ln(Q/pi^d)
+ sum_j Im lnGamma((1/2 + mu_j + it)/2):
   G(r) = sum_{0<g<=T2} e^{2i theta_L(g)} r^{-ig} = -eps c_L (rho_L(r)/sqrt(ab)) T2/2pi + o(T),  rho(1) = 1,
   c_L = B_L(1)/L(1, Ad) = lim F_L(1+x)/x, F_L(s) = sum conj(lambda(n)) mu_L(n) n^-s;  Hann weight sin^2(pi g/T2): half.
Products (finite-height mechanism, found post hoc in round 1, registered here for the first time): zeta_K = zeta L(rho):
   M1 = sum over all zeros of zeta_K of e^{2i theta_K} = -c_rho sum_{k<=T2/2pi} rho_rho(1/k);  M2 = sum over the rho-zeros of
   e^{2i theta_zeta} = -psi_rho(T2/2pi);  M3 = sum over the zeta-zeros of e^{2i theta_K} = 0.
   Each Artin factor also obeys the law: R = sum over the rho-zeros of e^{2i theta_rho} = -c_rho T2/2pi.
   L(C) = L(f) L(f^sigma) (genus-2 curves of GL(2) type): sum over all zeros of e^{i theta_C} = -(eps_a c_a + eps_b c_b) T2/2pi;
   with the product phase: 0.  Pairs L(E1) L(E2): the product phase on the union: 0.
