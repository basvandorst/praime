Round 117 tested Berry's per-prime number-variance formula dSigma2(1) = -(4/L^2) sum_k (ln p)^2 p^-k (1 - cos a_k)/a_k^2,
a_k = 2 pi k ln p / L, for the primes 2, 3, 5. The formula predicts effects of similar size for larger primes (at L = 9.38:
2: -0.0100, 3: -0.0122, 5: -0.0125, 7: -0.0116, 11: -0.0096, 13: -0.0089). Test on all fundamental discriminants with
100 <= |D| <= 400 (the 121 of rounds 115-116 plus the last third, offset 2, computed now; about 181 L-functions), zeros of
L(s, chi_D) up to 600, Sigma2(1) of the unfolded zeros with 100 <= gamma <= 600 on a dense grid of window starts (step
0.01 mean spacings); least squares on a constant and indicators of p | D for p = 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13; predictions averaged
over each group's L-functions' mean L.
Readings: (1) at least five of the six fitted coefficients are negative; (2) a straight-line fit of the six fitted
coefficients against the six predictions (through the origin) has slope between 0.7 and 1.6; (3) the coefficients for
7, 11, 13 pooled (their mean) are negative at more than 2 standard errors.
