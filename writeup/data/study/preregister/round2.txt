train window only. Two versions were tried there:
  v1 factorial-cumulant exponential (ln P = -A1/lam + A2/lam^2 - ...): converged to the wrong value (x1.6 at h=90);
     the cause is the diagonal X^2 = X, which the exponential formula for set functions ignores.
  v2 Bahadur form (theory2.Pattern): ln P(B) = ln S(B) - |B| ln lam + sum ln(1-p_m) + sum c~ - sum k3~ + sum k4~
     at order 4 it gave pred/actual 1.0009, 1.0007, 1.0021, 1.0026, 1.0083, 1.0086, 0.990 (se 0.0012..0.018).
v2 is frozen now. No parameter exists to fit; the only choices are the order and the cut-offs below.

Frozen choices:
  order 4 for patterns with span <= 120, order 3 above (cost); single-gap law summed to h = 12*lam_max + 40.
  joint law of two consecutive gaps: Pattern([0, h1, h1+h2]); classes as in probes.py (gap/2 capped at 20;
  gap/2 mod 3); pairs with h1 or h2 above the cap are obtained from marginals: P(h1, >=42) = P(h1) - sum P(h1,h2<=40).
  Variance of counts: exact HL pair sum, Var/mean = 1 - 1/lam + (2/(H lam)) sum_{h<H}(H-h)(S(0,h)-1).
