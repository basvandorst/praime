Round 89, pre-registered before the computation (commit order shows the order; STUDY.md section 1).

The k-th neighbour law for twin primes: the distance from a twin pair to the k-th next twin pair. Round 59's twin-gap
law is the z^0 coefficient of a generating function: in class a (a, a+2 coprime to 30030), with the interior twin
starts h (multiples of 6 in (0, d), possible in the class), their twin chances p_h = rho0^2 s_h and pair ratios R_hk of
r59.py (order 2, S_{>13} exact to 3000), G_a(z) = exp(sum ln(1 + p_h(z-1)) + 1/2 sum_{h!=k} R_hk w_h w_k) and
P(the k-th next twin starts at d) = rho0^2 S-ratio(d) [z^{k-1}] G_a(z), averaged over classes and over the window as in
round 59 (three lam values, weights e^u/u^2), 32-point Fourier sum, d <= 1200.
Data: twin pairs in [1e9, 2e9) (round 59's window), distances to the 2nd and 3rd next twin, conditioned on d <= 1200,
in round 59's 30 bands; the law normalised to the data's count within d <= 1200.
Readings: for k = 2 and k = 3, chi^2 below the 0.999 quantile of chi^2 with (bands used - 1) dof. Reported: the
same for the sum of k independent twin gaps from round 59's law (convolution) and k = 1 as a control.
