Why.  Rounds 115-116 found that each small prime dividing D lowers the spacing variance of the zeros by about 0.014;
the size was never explained.  The effective-matrix-size law of Bogomolny-Bohigas-Leboeuf-Monastra (2006), applied to
L(s, chi) with the bad-prime factors of Bogomolny-Keating (2013), shifts its constants by Lambda_S - Lambda =
sum_{p|k} ln^2 p/(p-1), Q_S - Q = -sum_{p|k} ln^3 p/(p-1)^2.  Derived here (derivation.md sections 2-3): these shifts
are exactly (L^2/2) x the variance and (L^3/6) x the third cumulant of the prime's explicit-formula density wave, and
CUE(N_eff) at the orders where the law is defined is a random dilation of the sine process (checked for n = 2..5), so
the law per prime is the slow-wave (dilation) limit, and it predicts NO change of the gap ratio (also not from the
Q-term).  At L = 9-11 the 1/L expansion fails (the 1/L^4 term of R2 exceeds the 1/L^2 term at unit distance; N_eff ~ 2).
The usable law is the same mechanism at finite wavelength.

The law (H_law; derivation.md section 5; conjecture: local universality, Bogomolny-Keating's random-matrix method with
the bad prime as short orbit): the zeros of L(s, chi_D) with p good are those with p missing, re-mapped by p's wave
  y = x + dN_p(x),  dN_p(x) = (1/pi) arg(1 - p^(-1/2) e^(i(k x + phi))),  k = 2 pi ln p / L,  L = ln(|D| t/2 pi),
phi uniform; everything else unchanged.  Gap statistics exact in the wave from the sine process's Janossy densities.
It reproduces the exact Bogomolny-Keating per-prime change of the number variance to 1-3 % for windows >= 1 spacing
(10-20 % low below 0.5) and the ratios-conjecture per-prime change of R3 to 11-31 % rms (L = 9.4-10.7).
Predictions at L = 9.4 (missing minus present), p = 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13:
  dVar(s) = -0.0126 -0.0144 -0.0136 -0.0121 -0.0097 -0.0087;   d<r~> = +0.0026 +0.0038 +0.0050 +0.0055 +0.0058 +0.0057.
A missing prime LOWERS the spacing variance and RAISES the gap ratio.  Level (no bad prime): the same second-order
formulas with the exact two-point function (known to overshoot the deviation from GUE by 15-30 %: zeta and R120).

Hypotheses (all parameter-free; per-L-function values in the JSON):
  H_law    the wave closure, sine base (frozen key 'law').
  H_law2   its second-order form with the exact two-point change (key 'law2'): part of the theory's own uncertainty.
  H_dil    slow waves only / effective CUE size per prime: the per-prime change is the dilation by the prime's density
           (key 'slow': Var change 1.29-1.40 x the law's; <r~> unchanged).
  H_bblm   BBLM's formula with Lambda_S, Q_S (key 'bblm'): Var = V0 - (2 Lambda_S/L^2 - 6 Q_S/L^3)(1 + V0) (the dilation
           with variance -2 Lambda_S/L^2 and third cumulant -6 Q_S/L^3); <r~> unchanged.
  H_0      bad primes ignored (key 'ignored').
  H_neg    a missing prime lowers <r~> (the scout's reading of the Q-term).

conjectural two- and three-point functions only (errors there 1-3 %, 10-20 %, 11-31 %, derivation 5.2); four variants
give the Var amplitude 0.90-1.00 of the law and the gap-ratio amplitude 0.94-1.17.  Stated: Var amplitude predicted to

Decisions (thresholds fixed now; primary = pooled P):
 D1 (spacing variance).  H_law passes if |gamma_V - 1| <= 4 se; if not, but gamma_V is inside the theory band
    [0.85, 1.05], the result is recorded as "consistent at the theory's stated accuracy" with its value (not a
    falsification); outside the band H_law's Var prediction is falsified.  H_dil is rejected if gamma_V <
    (1 + gamma_dil)/2 and gamma_dil - gamma_V >= 5 se.  H_0 is rejected if gamma_V >= 5 se.
 D2 (gap ratio; the reading that separates the law from slow waves / effective size).  "No change" (H_dil, H_bblm,
    H_0) is rejected if gamma_R >= 5 se and gamma_R > 0.5.  H_neg is rejected if gamma_R >= 5 se.  H_law passes if
    |gamma_R - 1| <= 4 se (theory band [0.85, 1.25] reported); H_law is falsified for the gap ratio if gamma_R < 0.5 or
    gamma_R > 1.6 with |gamma_R - 1| > 4 se.
 D3 (H_bblm, the effective-matrix-size law with the bad-prime constants).  Rejected if D2 rejects "no change", or if
    gamma_V < (1 + gamma_bblm)/2 and gamma_bblm - gamma_V >= 5 se (gamma_bblm 1.27 for P).  The chi2 of its Var pattern
    is reported, no decision (the law's own variants differ from each other by chi2 20-38 per set).
 D4 (levels, secondary).  Recorded against the expected offsets above; the level formula is falsified only if
    |mean(Var - law)| > 0.008 or |mean(r~ - law)| > 0.008.
 D5 (L-dependence, secondary).  H_law passes if |gamma_V(Bp) - gamma_V(B)| <= 4 se; "no L-dependence of the
    per-prime change" is rejected if (0.16 - difference) >= 5 se (expected se 0.028).
 Error rates: for D1/D2 under the noise model, < 1e-4 each way between the law and the hypotheses rejected; the bootstrap
 se replaces the model se in the decisions.  Every reading is also reported per set.
