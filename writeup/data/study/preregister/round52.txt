Entropy of the next prime gap (own derivation). To leading order the Hardy-Littlewood gap law is
P(g) = (S(0,g)/lam) e^(-g/lam) for even g, whose Shannon entropy is ln(e lam) - (1/2) mean_{g even}[S ln S]; with
S(0,g) = 2C2 prod_{p | g, p > 2} (p-1)/(p-2) and mean_even[S 1(p | g)] = 2/(p - 1) this is
  H(next gap) = log2(e lam) - K/ln 2 bits,  K = ln(2C2) + sum_{p>2} ln((p-1)/(p-2))/(p-1) = 0.7627474 nats
(brute force over even g <= 4e6: 0.762742). Relative to parity-only randomness the singular series removes
(K - ln 2)/ln 2 = 0.1004 bits. Corrections are expected to shrink with lam (like ln lam/lam).
