Law (heuristic, from Hardy-Littlewood; derivation.md): per admissible class b mod Q, X_b - E X_b = kappa Z_b + R_b with
kappa = S(2) phi(Q)/(A_Q ln p) (round 155, class form), Z_b = primes of class b plus primes of class b+2 weighted by
kappa; the HL moments give Var(kappa Z) from the pairs, Cov(X, kappa Z) from the triples (regression coefficient
beta_HL = 1.000), and the remainder R from the four-point sums minus both: P_rem = P_X - 2 P_cov + P_Z.  The linear term is
inside the four-point sums of rounds 42-43 (identity, derivation.md 1.3); the remainder's rigidity carries a ln^2 law,
leading -ln^2(W/Q)/(2 ln^2 x) (c_Q H_Q = 1).  Seen: the spread deficit of rounds 41-44 sits entirely in R; in sub-windows
R fits at every level; in the three seen decades the class deviations of R anti-correlate between sub-windows more than
HL says (twins only; h = 4..12 do not).

Decisions (fixed):
  P1: beta: mean over S0..S8 of the across-class regression coefficient of X on kappa Z, mod 30030 (1485 classes), equals
      mean beta_HL within 3 se, se = 1.17 * rms(nominal se)/3 (inflation 1.17 from 27 seen sub-windows); "independent"
      (beta 0) and "one member" (beta 0.5) rejected iff more than 5 se away
  P2: remainder at the fine level (30030 within 2310): mean over S0..S8 of measured/predicted within 3 * sqrt(2/1350)/3 of 1
  P3: linear part: mean over S0..S8 of u2_Z(30030)/P_Z within 3 * 1.08 * sqrt(2/1484)/3 of 1
  P4 (the open point): Z = (z_2310 + z_30030)/sqrt(2), z = (cross part measured - HL)/nominal sd of the remainder on D
      (decade spread minus the count-weighted sum of the sub-window spreads).  Z <= -3: excess anti-correlation of twin
      class deviations CONFIRMED (four-point remainder fails for twins in decade windows); Z >= -0.92: chance favoured;
      otherwise OPEN
  verdict: LAW CONFIRMED iff P1, P2, P3 pass; P4 is read separately with its three outcomes
  reported only: ratios of u2_X and u2_R at levels 1-3 for D and each S_s (mod 210 from the closed form, +-0.01)

Falsifier: if on the nine fresh sub-windows the class-level response coefficient misses beta_HL, or the fine-level
remainder misses 1, or the linear part misses its predicted size, each by more than 3 errors, the decomposition
"linear response + Hardy-Littlewood remainder" is falsified.
