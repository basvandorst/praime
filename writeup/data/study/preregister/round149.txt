Why. The CFKRS recipe (2005) and Heap's conjecture for non-primitive L-functions (arXiv 1303.6119, Conjecture 4) predict
  M_3 = int_100^1000 |L(1/2+it, chi_1) L(1/2+it, chi_2) L(1/2+it, chi_3)|^2 dt = int P(t) dt + O(T^(1/2+eps)),
  P(t) = sum over the 8 sets S of swapped L-functions (2.1) = sum_m h[m] prod_{j not in m} (ln(q_j t/2pi) + 2 gamma) (2.2),
  h(z) = prod_j c_j(2 z_j) prod_{i<j} L(1 + z_i + z_j, chi_i chi_j)^2 A_3(z), h(0) = a_L(1) (Heap's arithmetic factor,
  with prod_{i<j} L(1, chi_i chi_j)^2 and the three-body Euler factor A_3).
  No asymptotic is proved for any product of three L-functions (degree 3, as the sixth moment of zeta). For two different
  characters the same recipe is a theorem (Topacogullari, Math. Z. 2021, Thm 1.3; our polynomial equals his to 3e-12).
  No numerical test of the k = 3 conjecture (or of the k = 2 theorem) was found in print.

What is conjecture (the filter). The order T(log T)^3 is theorem-grade (lower bound unconditional, upper bound under GRH,
  Hagen 2024); readings that only see "M_3 grows with h(0)" test almost nothing. Conjectural, and read here:
  (a) the level: the universal factor 1 together with the off-diagonal (mixed swaps |S| = 1, 2) and the lower-order
      terms; the diagonal-only alternative (AFE diagonal, H_diag) is 11.3 % higher (mean over the test triples; 7.2 .. 27.1 %);
  (b) the three-body Euler factor A_3/prod A_ij (0.28 .. 2.45 over the test triples, median 1.10), absent under H_no3
      and H_kirk.
  Pair readings and the theorem controls test nothing new; they calibrate.

Hypotheses (all parameter-free; densities in derivation.md 2.4-2.5):
  H_full  the recipe (2.2).
  H_diag  the mean square of the diagonal of the product's approximate functional equation (sharp cutoff at sqrt(C)):
          zero and full swaps only; leading term h(0)(sum L_j)^3/24.
  H_no3   the recipe with A_3(z) replaced by prod_{i<j} A_ij(z_i + z_j) (no three-body Euler factor).
  H_kirk  pairwise superposition: prod_{i<j} P_2(chi_i, chi_j) / prod_j P_1(chi_j) (built from the theorem-grade
          pair and single formulas).
  H_ind   independence: prod_j P_1(chi_j) (no coupling factor at all).
  H_lead  the leading term h(0) prod_j ln(q_j t/2pi) only.

Theory's own uncertainty (not in the bands). (i) The recipe's remainder, conjectured O(T^(1/2+eps)), is the noise
  itself (20-24 % per triple at these heights, 7 % per pair, 1 % per single); its mean over characters is conjectured 0.
  A finite-height mean offset is not excluded by anything: allowance A = max(0.03, 3 |rho_2 - 1|), calibrated by the pair
  control on the same characters and heights. (ii) H_diag is defined only up to the AFE weight: about +-2 % (derivation
  2.5); it does not move the decision (H_diag sits 11.3 % above H_full). (iii) Truncations (Euler product 1e5, Gauss
  quadrature) < 1e-7; quadrature of the data < 2e-5.

Decisions (thresholds fixed now):
 D1 (three-body factor; primary falsifier). H_full passes if |gamma_no3 - 1| <= 4 se. H_no3 is rejected if gamma_no3 >= 5 se
    and gamma_no3 >= 0.5; the same with kirk for H_kirk.
 D2 (level; mixed swaps). H_full passes if |theta - 1| <= 4 se. H_diag is rejected if theta <= theta_diag - 5 se, where
    theta_diag = mean(P_diag/P_full) over the triples (1.1132). If H_full fails D2 with |theta - 1| <= A it is recorded as a
    finite-height level offset (sign and size reported), not a falsification; if |theta - 1| > A the recipe's level is
    wrong at these heights.
 D3 (independence, leading order only). H_ind and H_lead rejected if gamma_ind >= 5 se, resp. gamma_lead >= 5 se.
 D4 (pair control). If |rho_2 - 1| > 4 se_2, the pair formula (a theorem's main term) is off at these heights by more
    than the noise: the CV reading theta is then reported but D2 uses the raw mean of r instead, and the round records
    that the controls failed.
 Reported in all cases: C1-C3, P1, T3, T4, T5, every jackknife se, the per-character table, the excluded characters.
