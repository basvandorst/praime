Information law for the next gap (own derivation). Given p mod q for all primes q <= y, the positions of the y-rough
numbers after p are fixed; only K, the index of the next prime among them (K = 1: the first rough number is prime), is
unknown. If each rough number is prime with chance rho = hit(y) (round 45, corrected formula of r45b.json), independently,
K is geometric: P(K = k) = rho (1 - rho)^(k-1), and the remaining uncertainty about the next gap is
  H(next gap | p mod q <= y) = H(K) = h2(rho)/rho bits,  h2(r) = -r log2 r - (1 - r) log2(1 - r).
Data: the windows of round 45; K counted per gap as 1 + the composites with smallest prime factor > y; H(K) by plug-in
with the Miller-Madow correction; the same grid of y. Reported: measured H(K) and P(K = 2)/P(K = 1) against the geometric
law. Reading fixed now: the law holds if |H measured - h2(rho)/rho| <= 0.05 bits at every grid point with u >= 2.5;
deviations show the correlations between successive rough numbers (Hardy-Littlewood), which the law neglects.
