Mutual information between consecutive prime gaps (own derivation). To leading order the joint law of consecutive gaps
is proportional to S({0, g1, g1+g2}) e^(-(g1+g2)/lam) and each marginal to S({0, g}) e^(-g/lam), so
I(g_n; g_{n+1}) -> I_inf = E[ln(S3/(S2(g1) S2(g2)))], which factorises over primes by CRT:
  I_inf = sum_{p >= 3} I_p,  I_p = (p-1)/p^2 f_A ln((1-1/p)/(1-2/p)) + (p-1)(p-2)/p^2 f_B ln((1-3/p)(1-1/p)/(1-2/p)^2),
  f_A = (1-2/p)/(1-1/p)^3, f_B = (1-3/p)/(1-1/p)^3;  I_inf = 0.1869077 nats = 0.26965 bits (p = 3 alone: 0.25 bits;
checked against direct enumeration of the local sums for p <= 61).
