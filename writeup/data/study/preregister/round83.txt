The full distribution of the distance to the k-th neighbour, D_k = p_{n+k} - p_n, from round 81's law (same formula,
y = 13, order 2, 48-point Fourier sum, lam = ln(1.05e9), D <= 240), against the primes in [1e9, 1.1e9), for
k = 2, 3, 4. Bands of width 12 (D in [2,12], [14,24], ..., [230,240]); bands with expected count below 50 are pooled
into the last kept band; the law is normalised to the data's count within D <= 240.
Reading: for each k, chi^2 below the 0.999 quantile of chi^2 with (bands - 1) degrees of freedom. Reported alongside:
chi^2 of the Gamma(k) law with mean k lam (Gallagher's Poisson model) and of the plain sum of k independent gaps from
the class-averaged gap law (no residue memory at all).
