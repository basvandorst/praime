[1e11, 1e11 + 1e10) (never used for twins). Law: r59.py unchanged (y = 13, order 2), lam over the window. Bands fixed
now, the same as round 59's: 6-30, 36-60, ..., 576-600 (width 24, step 30) and 606-660, ..., 1146-1200 (width 54, step
60), 30 bands; the tail beyond 1200 reported separately. Reading fixed now: the law holds if chi^2 over the 30 bands is
below dof + 3 sqrt(2 dof) = 53.2; the tail is reported against the law's complement (round 59's truncation bias is
expected to show there again, larger relative to the tail at this scale since more mass lies beyond 1200).
