Round 136, pre-registered (commit order shows the order; STUDY.md section 1). When this file was committed, zeros of

Why. In test B of round 134 (184 real L-functions, 400 < |D| <= 700, heights 100-600), the amplitude of the bad-prime
off-diagonal term of Bogomolny-Keating (BK) came out at gamma = 0.932 +- 0.024 (two-sided p = 0.003 against 1). Five
explanations were worked out (derivation.md):
  (a) lower-order terms (ratios conjecture with the exact gamma factor and parity);
  (b) density drift across a window;
  (c) the bad-prime factor for k not squarefree;
  (d) the unfolding and the estimator;
  (e) anything else.
None gives more than 1e-4 in gamma; (c) gives exactly 0.
  - The ratios-conjecture two-point function for primitive chi mod k equals BK's term by term: the bad-prime factor
    (p/(p-1))^2 comes from the residue of L(1+z, chi_0), and the difference is 1e-12 numerically.
  - The X-factor has modulus 1, so there is no amplitude correction. The phase differs from the test's 2 pi r by
    -eps/(24 t^2) (2e-5 rad).
  - The parity enters only through a constant +-1/8 in N(t).
RETROSPECTIVE evidence (data known; not blind):
  - The same statistic on the 181 real L-functions of rounds 117/120 (100 <= |D| <= 400, the same heights 100-600) gives
    gamma = 1.001 +- 0.019.
  - Inside B, the part of the per-L-function off pattern that the prime indicators do not carry has slope
    1.042 +- 0.028, against 0.938 +- 0.021 for the indicator part (z = -2.9). B's registered reading (6) is
    0.975 +- 0.014.
  - Joint chi2 of these four numbers: BK 11.7 on 4 (p 0.019), "plain amplitude 0.932" 32.5 (p 1.5e-6), "off-diagonal at
    L + 0.3" 34.0 (p 7.5e-7).
The proposer's reading: BK is complete, and B's indicator contrasts made a low excursion. This round tests that, blind,
against the two phenomenological alternatives and against a descriptive conductor trend.

Hypotheses (all parameter-free except H_k, which is a descriptive fit to retrospective data):
  H_BK   the BK law as frozen in round 134 (lowzero/bk/). Every amplitude reading is 1.
  H_AMP  BK with the bad-prime off-diagonal change multiplied by 0.932. Every amplitude reading is 0.932.
  H_LP3  BK with the bad-prime off-diagonal change evaluated at L + 0.3 (diagonal at L). Readings about 0.92-0.94, listed
         per sample below.
  H_k    gamma falls with the conductor: 1.001, 1.017, 0.956 and 0.917 in the four half-samples (rounds 117/120 split at
         |D| = 248, B split at 551) give slope -0.058 +- 0.035 per unit ln|D| (1.7 sigma; no mechanism known).
  H_set  the deficit belongs to test B's 184 L-functions (their between-group slope 0.938), not to the law.

Decisions (thresholds fixed now; 0.966 is halfway between 1 and 0.932):
 D1 (amplitude; primary). Take the inverse-variance mean of R2 over the tests that are run (C alone, or C + Dn).
      >= 0.966 : H_AMP and H_LP3 rejected for the fresh data;
      <  0.966 : the BK amplitude rejected, and the proposer's reading ("B was an excursion") killed.
    Error rates under the noise model:
      C alone:  0.034 each way between BK and AMP; LP3 above the line 0.022;
      C + Dn:   0.005 each way; LP3 0.0025.
    The same rule on R1 has error rates 0.098 (C) and 0.035 (C + Dn); report it, but D1 decides on R2.
 D2 (structure). In each fresh test, |between - within| <= 2.5 null sd (C 0.105, Dn 0.093, Bp 0.12) is expected under
    H_BK, H_AMP and H_LP3 alike: a change of the law moves both parts. A failure would repeat B's pattern, and would mean
    the indicator contrasts carry something no amplitude or L-shift describes.
 D3 (conductor). If R1(Dn) <= 0.95 while R1(C) >= 0.97: H_k supported, and BK is in question for |D| >~ 500 at heights
    below 600; this also kills the proposer's reading. Under H_BK the chance of R1(Dn) <= 0.95 is 0.03. Under H_k it is
    0.75, using the trend error 0.039 and the se 0.027. If R1(Dn) >= 0.97, H_k loses its support. That is only about a
    1 sigma statement, because the trend itself is uncertain.
 D4 (set, only if Bp is run). R3 between(Bp) <= 0.95 supports H_set; >= 0.97 rejects it. Weak: 2 sigma separation
    (0.938 against 1, se 0.031).
 Reported in all cases: R4, R5, the level reading of r134b.py (c), the completeness numbers.

Statuses:
  - The two-point function is Bogomolny-Keating's (heuristic), equal to the ratios-conjecture function (Conrey-Farmer-
    Zirnbauer recipe, as applied by Conrey-Snaith). That equality for p | k, the exponent independence, the exact-phase
    correction -eps/(24 t^2), and the between/within reading are derived here (derivation.md sections 1-5).
  - The retrospective numbers (1.001 on rounds 117/120; between 0.938 and within 1.042 in B; the joint chi2) were computed
    on data already known and are not evidence of the kind this round seeks.
  - H_AMP and H_LP3 are phenomenological; H_k is a descriptive fit with no mechanism.
