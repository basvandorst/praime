Why. Round 141 found that the diagonal cross term of two L-functions is a consequence of the explicit formula, and that
the one conjectural term, the recipe's swapped term with the phase e^{2i(theta2(t2) - theta1(t1))} and the factor
H12 = L(1 - iu, psi) L(1 + iu, conj psi) A12(-iu), failed its registered window reading because the predictor was
computed wrongly. The review's phase-cancelling statistic X12 gave 1.004 +- 0.007 of the recipe on the 17,020 used pairs,
retrospectively. This round tests it where it was never computed: 546 real L-functions among themselves (148,785 pairs),
the same 181 at new heights, and 88 complex characters (both half-lines), where the root numbers enter.

The law (conjecture: ratios recipe; derivation.md section 3). chi1 mod q1, chi2 mod q2 distinct primitive, parities a_j,
kappa_j = q_j/phi(q_j), eps_j = tau(chi_j)/(i^a_j sqrt q_j), theta_j(t) = (t/2) ln(q_j/pi) + Im lnGamma((1/2 + a_j + it)/2),
psi = chi1 conj chi2 (psi(p) = 0 for p | q1 q2), K(u) = exp(-u^2/2 sigma^2):
   X12 = (1/T) sum_{g1, g2} e^{2i theta1(g1) - 2i theta2(g2)} K(g2 - g1)  (window-smoothed in the midpoint, swap2_test.py),
   E X12 = (1/4 pi^2) kappa1 kappa2 eps1 conj(eps2) J12,  J12 = int H12(u) K(u) du,
   H12(u) = prod_{p not | q1 q2} (1 - 2/p + y)/((1 - conj y)(1 - 1/p)^2), y = psi(p) p^{iu}/p.
  One-sided functions: real chi_D (gamma > 0); complex chi (gamma > 0) and conj chi (the mirrored gamma < 0), eps(conj chi)
  = conj eps(chi). Real pairs: eps1 conj eps2 = 1, J real. (chi, conj chi): eps_chi^2, psi = chi^2 (the zeros of one
  L-function at +t against -t; no pole). J from the Euler product to 3e4 on u in [0, 6] step 0.05: within 7.7e-5 of PARI's
  exact L(1 - iu, psi*) on 26 pairs (relative 4e-5); quadrature error < 2e-8.
One-point law (theorem in substance, derivation.md section 2): sum_{T1 < g <= T2} e^{2i theta_chi(g)} = -eps kappa (T2 - T1)/2pi
  + o(T); per function it equals the prime sum R_w = -(i^a/sqrt q) sum_n chi(n) Lambda(n) e(-n/q) w(2 pi n/q) to 0.2 % of
  its size (Hann taper; Fn: rms |G - R| 0.23, slope 1.0000 +- 0.0001). Its readings are DATA CHECKS, with frozen values.

Hypotheses (parameter-free):
  H_law    the law above (all slope readings 1; Im readings 0; no-root coefficient 0).
  H_half   the swapped term at half its size (slopes 0.5).
  H_none   no swapped term (slopes 0): independent Gram modulations absent.
  H_const  H12 replaced by 1: the product of the two one-point terms (independently modulated Gram lattices).
  H_noroot (complex) the root-number factor eps1 conj(eps2) dropped.
  H_conjJ  (complex) the other pairing convention, J -> conj J (differs only through Im J).
