Round 139 (direction map of the within part), pre-registered before the computation (commit order shows the
order; STUDY.md section 1). No statistic below has been computed on the fresh windows or on the fresh response
stretches. The numbers marked "seen" come from windows that were already used. Code: writeup/code/study/map/.

Why. Round 137 and the forty-fourth review left the growth of the within part with the block length open: per unit
ln H the within part of the k = 1 table mod 5 falls about twice as fast as the linear-response (pair-correlation) tail,
and the review placed the excess in the four mixed directions mod 5 (the order-4 character against the Legendre
symbol): -0.0083 +- 0.0021 per unit ln H per direction over both heights, unexplained. This round asks two things on
fresh primes: (1) is the mod-5 mixed excess there, with the size seen; (2) do the other cells of a map over eight
moduli and all direction types, whose large values were selected on seen data, keep their sign.

INSTRUMENT (map.py, self-contained, numpy + standard library; nothing imported from the repo; evaluate.py applies the
readings below mechanically to map.py's report)
  Window W = [x0, x0 + 1e8); blocks of length H = 1e4, 1e5, 1e6 by the position of p_n; c_n = class of p_n mod q;
  table X_ab = #{n : p_n in the block, c_n = a, c_(n+k) = b}; per window P^ = pooled table row-normalised;
  E = N_a P^_ab; y = (X - E)/sqrt(E) in R^(C^2), C = phi(q).
  Directions: for each pair of non-principal characters (chi_i, chi_j) of (Z/q)*, the function chi_i(a) conj chi_j(b)
  weighted by sqrt(pi_a P_ab), projected on the within space (row and column parts removed), real and imaginary parts,
  Loewdin-orthonormalised (P, pi pooled over the 24 windows). (C - 1)^2 real directions d.
  W_d(H) = sum over blocks (y . B_d)^2 / (NB - 1) per window. Measured slope s_d = (W_d(1e6) - W_d(1e4)) / ln 100.
  Comparator (linear response, the review's tailemp.py per direction): the response of the table to inserting a prime
  at a site x coprime to 510510 (k = 1: +(L1, x) +(x, R1) -(L1, R1); k = 2: +(L2, x) +(L1, R1) +(x, R2) -(L2, R1)
  -(L1, R2)), averaged per class u mod Q = lcm(510510, q) on the response stretches; A_d(h) = mean_u r_ud r_(u+h)d
  (lag 0 from cross products of disjoint quarters, so no sampling-noise term); W_tail,d(H) = (2/lam)(Q/phi(Q))
  (lam_resp/lam) sum_(h<H) (1 - h/H)(1 - e^(-h/lam)) A_d(h mod Q)(S_(>17)(h) - 1); LR slope_d = (W_tail,d(1e6) -
  W_tail,d(1e4)) / ln 100 (y = 17: the review's machinery).
  EXCESS of a set of directions = mean over its directions and over the 24 windows of (s_d - LR slope_d).
  Error = jackknife over the 24 windows (measurement) combined in quadrature with the jackknife over the four quarters
  of the response stretches (comparator). Both are measurement errors; no model error enters any band.
  Direction types: eq (i = j), conj (i = conj j, i != j), rr (both real, different), rc (one real, one complex),
  cc (both complex, neither equal nor conjugate); subtypes by (ord chi_i, ord chi_j, ord chi_i conj chi_j).
  Moduli q = 3, 5, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13; k = 1, 2; controls q = 5, 7 at k = 50.

    to -0.004 per direction at k = 1, so the measured slopes are biased by +0.0002 to +0.0008 (towards zero), not
    towards the excess.

  - The 1e4-blocks in random order (E[slope] = 0 exactly): rms z 1.06 over types, 1.09 over 322 groups (25 groups

Known weak points, stated now:
- The comparator is the linear response at y = 17. It grows with y (from 11 to 13 to 17 by 6-33 % per step, the steps
  shrinking but not converged), so an excess common to all directions is expected from the truncation alone; that is
  why S0 (a contrast inside mod 5) is registered next to P1. The truncation ratio y = 13 / y = 17 is 0.86 for the mixed
  directions mod 5, 0.87 and 0.79 for the others, but nothing beyond y = 17 was computed.
- The comparator's estimator matters when the classes are thin: cross products of disjoint parts at every lag depend
  on how a single stretch is split (mod 5, all directions: -0.0052 to -0.0057 per direction against -0.0052 for the
  pooled class means), and for q = 8, 9 by 30 % to a factor 2.8 in single types; with 24 stretches all estimators
  agree. The registered estimator (pooled class means, lag 0 from quarters) is the one that matches the review and
  the 24-window responses.
- V is rescaled by lam_resp/lam, which is wrong in general (V falls about like lam^-1.9, see the validation); the
  fresh response stretch lies 0.07 below the windows in lam, so the error is below 0.5 % of the comparator.
- With 24 windows the jackknife error has 23 degrees of freedom; single groups (not types) show slightly heavier tails
  than normal in the block-shuffle control.
- The secondary cells were selected on the seen map (selection bias); their powers are upper bounds.
- The O(1/N) bias of the statistic (from the chain null) moves the slopes by at most +0.0008 per direction, towards
  zero; it is not subtracted.
