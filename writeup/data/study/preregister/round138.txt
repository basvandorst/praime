orbits in the three-point function. Committed with predictions_kappa3.json before any zero of the fresh tests was
computed. When this file was committed: no zero of Fn or Bp existed; on the zero sets of rounds 117-136 (R120, B, Dn, C

Derivation and code: writeup/code/study/lowzero/triple/ (derivation.md, lib/tplib.py, kappa3_tables.py, predict3.py,
power3.py).

Why. The physics picture: zeros of L(s, chi) are levels of a chaotic system whose periodic orbits are the prime powers;
an orbit p | k is missing. Rounds 117-136 confirmed the two-point function with the missing orbits (Bogomolny-Keating
2013; amplitude 0.998 +- 0.014 on fresh data). The three-point function with all arithmetic terms is known for zeta
(Bogomolny-Keating; Conrey-Snaith, ratios conjecture) and was compared with zeta's zeros once (Conrey-Snaith 2007,
100,000 zeros); for Dirichlet L-functions, and for the conductor's primes, no test was found in print. The statistic:
kappa3(l) = E[(n - l)^3], n = number of unfolded zeros in a window of l mean spacings; kappa3 = k[3] + 3 Sigma2 - 2l with
k[3] = the cube integral of the connected three-point function T3.

The law (derivation.md sections 3-5; conjecture: ratios recipe = BK 2013 form, checked equal to 1e-7):
  T3 = (1/L^3) sum of six terms J*_k(a; b1, b2) = Q_k - P1_k P3_k - P1_k P3_k, L = ln(k t/2pi), every prime p | k deleted
  from every prime sum and Euler product, zeta(1 +- x) -> (k/phi(k)) L(1 +- x, chi_0), zeta'/zeta -> L'/L(., chi_0).
  A prime p | k:  (1) removes the triple-orbit term (orbits p^m, p^n, p^(m+n)), which in kappa3 is exactly
        dkappa3_diag(l; p, L) = +(6/pi^3) sum_{m,n>=1} sin(m th) sin(n th) sin((m+n) th) / (m n (m+n) p^(m+n)), th = pi l ln p/L
      (leading term (3/pi^3) sin^2(th) sin(2 th)/p^2; +0.0166 for p = 2 near l = 4);
  (2) multiplies the off-diagonal amplitude by BK's two-point factor and shifts the bracket P3 (derivation.md 3.3); in
      kappa3 this part, 3 dSigma2 + dk[3]_P, is small (-0.007 .. +0.006 for p = 2), because the large drop of Sigma2
      (3 dSigma2 down to -0.33) is cancelled inside the three-point function: a missing orbit changes the variance at
      order ln^2 p/p but the skewness only at order 1/p^2 plus this remainder.
  The level for k = 1: kappa3 - kappa3_GUE = -0.006 .. -0.025 for l = 0.5 .. 6 (L = 9.4 .. 10.7); kappa3_GUE is
  +0.045, +0.011, ..., +0.002 (l = 0.5, 1, ..., 8; CUE-checked).

Hypotheses (all parameter-free):
  H_full  the law above. All amplitude readings 1.
  H_GUE   kappa3 = kappa3_GUE for every L-function (no arithmetic in the skewness).
  H_Q0    the conductor does not remove the triple-orbit term (Q over all primes, everything else as H_full).
  H_2pt   bad primes act through the two-point function only (k[3] of k = 1 at the same L, Sigma2 with the bad primes).
  H_diag  bad primes act on kappa3 only through the triple-orbit term (kappa3 of k = 1 at the same L plus dkappa3_diag):
          tests the off-diagonal remainder 3 dSigma2 + dk[3]_P (the bad-prime factors of P1 and P3).

k[3]_i(l) = kappa3_i - 3 var(c) + 2 mean(c); l = 0.5, 1, ..., 8. Edge check (reported, no reading): the same with
x = arange(u_0 + 3, u_end - 3 - l, 0.01).
