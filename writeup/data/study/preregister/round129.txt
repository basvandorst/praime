The zeros' rigidity as the sum of all primes' waves (Berry 1988, classical; never computed in this repo). With
L = ln(t/2pi), the form factor is the diagonal sum over prime powers below e^L plus saturation at 1 beyond (tau > 1):
  Sigma2_Berry(l) = (2/pi^2) [ sum_{n <= e^L} Lambda(n)^2/(n ln^2 n) sin^2(pi l ln n/L)  +  int_1^inf sin^2(pi l tau)/tau^2 dtau ],
against GUE: Sigma2_GUE(l) = (1/pi^2) [ln(2 pi l) + gamma + 1 - Ci(2 pi l)]. Data: zeta's zeros with 7.6 <= L <= 8.6
(26,812 of the first 40,000), unfolded with N(t) = (t/2pi) ln(t/2pi e) + 7/8, Sigma2(l) on a dense grid (step 0.01),
l = 0.5, 1, ..., 8; Berry's formula at the mean L of the zeros used.
Readings: (1) for l >= 2 Berry's formula is nearer the data than GUE at every l; (2) the largest |Berry - data| over
l = 1 ... 8 is below 0.02; (3) reported: the data minus GUE at l = 8 (the saturation).
