Round 125, pre-registered before the computation (commit order shows the order; STUDY.md section 1).

The forward direction of round 124: do low zeros predict prime-rich quadratics? 2,000 random positive fundamental
discriminants d in [1e5, 1e6] (seed 125); scaled first zero g of L(s, chi_d). Group LOW = the 15 with the smallest g;
group RANDOM = 15 others drawn at random from the rest (seed 125). For each d: the longest run of consecutive y in
[-80, 80] with |A y^2 + B y + C| prime, over all forms with B^2 - 4AC = d (or 4d when d = 0 mod 4 is not the case for the
form) with 1 <= A <= 300 and 0 <= B < 2A (B^2 = d mod 4A), as in the quadratic search (no distinctness filter).
Readings: (1) the median best run of LOW exceeds that of RANDOM by at least 5; (2) a one-sided Mann-Whitney test
LOW > RANDOM has p < 0.01.
