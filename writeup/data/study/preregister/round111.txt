Rounds 104-107 for degree 2 (cubic fields). For every S3 cubic field K with |disc| <= 5000, both signatures (PARI nflist),
the Artin L-function L = zeta_K/zeta (degree 2, conductor |disc|, gamma shifts (0,0) for totally real K, (0,1) for complex
K). A cubic is prime-rich when many small primes are inert (no root mod p). Quantities: gamma_1 = lowest positive zero of
L (lfunzeros); L(1); P_K = least prime p not dividing disc at which the nflist polynomial has a root mod p;
S = 2 L'/L(1) + ln(N/pi^2) + sum_j psi((1 + mu_j)/2), which equals sum_rho 1/(rho(1-rho)) (Hadamard, as fact 2049);
g = gamma_1 ln|disc| / (2 pi).
Readings, separately for each signature: (1) Spearman(ln L(1), g) >= 0.4; (2) Spearman(ln P_K, g) <= -0.3;
(3) the first zero predicted from S as in round 104 (the pair +-gamma_1 takes the excess of S over the median of fields
with |ln|disc'| - ln|disc|| < 0.25: 2/(1/4 + g^2) = S - S_med + 2/(1/4 + g_med^2)) has Spearman >= 0.9 with the true one.
