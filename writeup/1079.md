# Puzzle 1079: Forest of prime numbers

> **In short:** Q1 asks whether 13, 23 and 31 are the best last primes for n = 1, 2, 3 stones in the prime stepping-stones game; Q2 asks for n > 3. An exhaustive search of the game tree (stones placed lazily, which is equivalent to placing them in advance) proves 13, 23, 31 optimal and gives the maxima 41, 53, 61, 73, 89 for n = 4 … 8. The full analysis, shared with puzzle 1085, is in [1085.md](1085.md).
