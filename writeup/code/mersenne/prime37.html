Prime gaps

Walk along the primes 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, … and write down each step: prime 1 to 2 (2 to 3) has gap 1, prime 2 to 3 (3 to 5) gap 2, prime 3 to 4 (5 to 7) gap 2, prime 4 to 5 (7 to 11) gap 4. Every gap after the first is even, because all primes after 2 are odd. Below: a bar for every gap, a mosaic with one tile per gap, how often each gap occurs, and the full list.
