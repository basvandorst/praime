Prime gears

The numbers from 1 up as gears: the first 30, 100 or 250. Gear k has k teeth, and one tick moves every gear one tooth, so gear k is back home every k ticks. At tick t the gears at home are exactly the divisors of t, and t is prime when only gear 1 and gear t are home. Press Start and watch.

Speed: 1 ticks per second
