Prime high jump

Between 113 and 127 there are no primes: a gap of 14, longer than any gap before it. Every such new longest gap is a world record in the high jump of the primes. The 85 records known (OEIS A005250) reach a gap of 1,854 after a prime of 21 digits. Cramér guessed in 1936 that the bar never goes much above ln² p. Impostors: prime24, window: prime22, turtle (the same regular gaps): prime8, baskets: prime16.
