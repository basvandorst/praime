Mersenne exponents among the numbers

Every number from 1 to 500,000 is a dot: row by row in a square, wound into a spiral, stacked as a tree or a triangle, or along a space-filling curve. The pink dots are the Mersenne exponents among them (the k with 2k − 1 prime), and the lines link them. In the square a column is a remainder, k mod the width; in the tree the centre line holds the numbers m² + (m + 1)², and 5, 13, 61 and 9,941 sit on it (3.1 expected by chance).
