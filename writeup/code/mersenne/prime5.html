Prime balance

A balance that receives weights one by one: the primes (or all numbers), each placed on the left or the right pan by a rule you choose. Press Start and watch which side is heavier, how often the pans are exactly equal, and when the lead changes. Some rules hide famous surprises. Beads: prime4, gears: prime3, a race with more horses: prime6.

Weights per second: 20

What happened

    Every time the pans are equal

    Backtest: a run to 5,000, checked to 1,000,000

    Every rule with the weight 1 each, first over the numbers up to 5,000, then up to 1,000,000 (for the Mersenne exponents: the 20 up to 5,000, then all 52). The table says which pan was heavier most of the time, and whether that still holds in the long run. 14 of the 18 rules keep the side that is heavier most of the time (two of them trivially: in turn and on the lighter pan). All 104 runs (every rule, source and weight) were done twice, by this page and by a separate program, with the same counts, totals, equal moments and lead changes.
