Prime balance

A balance that receives weights one by one: the primes (or all numbers), each placed on the left or the right pan by a rule you choose. Press Start and watch which side is heavier, how often the pans are exactly equal, and when the lead changes. Some rules hide famous surprises. Beads: prime4, gears: prime3.

Weights per second: 20

What happened

    Every time the pans are equal
