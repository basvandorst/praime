Prime beads

The numbers 1 to 200 as beads, the primes lit. Grab a bead and drag it: its neighbours come along, as if tied with elastic. The elastic is tied in the shape you start from (or the shape you have when you change the neighbours), so nothing moves until you drag. Shape the string as you like and watch the lit beads. Does a pattern appear in the rest? The meter under the picture says whether the primes lie closer together than random groups of beads. Double-click a bead (or hold it on a phone) to pin it. Gears: prime3, layouts: prime2, a balance: prime5.
