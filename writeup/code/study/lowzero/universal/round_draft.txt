Why. Round 148 found, on 158 elliptic curves, sum_{T1<g<=T2} e^{2i theta_E(g)} = -w c_E (T2-T1)/2pi, c_E = B_E/L(1,Sym^2 E).
general_law.md derives the same law for every primitive L-function by the same recipe steps, none of which uses the
degree:  sum e^{2i theta_L(g)} = -eps_L c_L (T2-T1)/2pi + o(T),  c_L = lim F_L(1+x)/x = B_L(1)/L(1, Ad L),
F_L(s) = sum conj(lambda(n)) mu_L(n) n^-s, lines at r = a/b with factors rho_L(r)/sqrt(ab); c_L = 0 for products.
Degree 1 is a theorem (c = q/phi(q), checked here with complex characters). A first test (this folder, predictions frozen
before any zero was computed, sha256 946e1e3a1436dd78e87fb53bccded7ec1b93d46a27364d2622ce3668af3bdf38) gave on 20 primitive

zeta_K for d = -23 NOT 0 (-62.1 against 0, 3.2 block se), d = -31 -25.4 (1.2 se). Post hoc a mechanism was found
(general_law.md section 6.3): a degree-1 factor's chirp meets the lines r = 1/k of the other factor with aligned phases,
giving the finite-height term -c_rho sum_{k<=T/2pi} rho_rho(1/k) (o(T), but large at T = 300 when small primes do not
split), and -psi_rho(T/2pi) for the zeta phase alone (measured +11.5 against +12.0). This round tests the law blind on
new L-functions in every degree and registers the product mechanism.

The law (frozen form). L primitive, analytic normalisation, Q conductor, mu_j gamma shifts, eps root number (PARI
lfunrootres; Lambda(s) = eps conj-Lambda(1-s)); theta_L(t) = (t/2) ln(Q/pi^d) + sum_j Im lnGamma((1/2 + mu_j + it)/2):
   G(r) = sum_{0 < g <= T2} e^{2i theta_L(g)} r^{-ig} = -eps c_L (rho_L(r)/sqrt(ab)) T2/2pi + o(T)   (rho(1) = 1),
   Hann weight sin^2(pi g/T2): half.  c_L: B_L(1)/L(1, Ad) where PARI gives L(1, Ad) (Sym^2, Sym^4, Sym^6 of E; Artin; level-1
   Sym^2 built with exact coefficients), else the direct product (exact local factors p <= 3000, first order to 1e5,
   log-Cesaro, bias nu/(sqrt P ln P)); accuracy 0.1 % (the two routes agree to 0.06 % in this folder).
Products (registered mechanism): for zeta_K = zeta L(rho) (K a non-normal cubic field):
   M1 = sum_{all zeros of zeta_K} e^{2i theta_K(g)} = -c_rho sum_{k <= T2/2pi} rho_rho(1/k) + o(T^{1/2+eps}),
   M2 = sum_{rho zeros} e^{2i theta_zeta(g)} = -psi_rho(T2/2pi) + O(T^{1/2} log^2 T),
   M3 = sum_{zeta zeros} e^{2i theta_K(g)} = small (degree-2 chirp, no alignment).
For L(E1) L(E2) (two curves, union of zeros, phase theta_1 + theta_2): 0 (degree-2 chirps only).

Hypotheses.
  H1        the law (all slopes 1, Im 0), lines with rho_L, products by the mechanism.
  H0        no main term (slopes 0).
  H-noroot  -c T/2pi without eps (distinguishes eps = -1 and complex eps).

  H-c1      c_L = 1 for every L (amplitude does not follow B/L(1, Ad)).
  H-prod0   products: M1 = 0 (the recipe's leading order only).
