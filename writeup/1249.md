# Puzzle 1249: Cycles of p ↦ GPD(2p + 3)

> **In short:** The page asks whether p ↦ GPD(2p + 3) has cycles other than 5 → 13 → 29 → 61 and 43 → 89 → 181 → 73 → 149. On a cycle, 2p_i + 3 = s_i·p_(i+1), and the odd cofactors multiply to S = ∏(2 + 3/p_i) ≥ 2^L + 1. That forces a new cycle with least prime above X to have length L with 2^L((1 + 3/(2X))^L − 1) ≥ 1. Every prime below 2·10⁸ ends in a known cycle (exhaustive), so a new cycle has length ≥ 23, and ≥ 28 given the page's search to 10¹⁰. The analysis, which covers puzzle 1250 too, is in [1250.md](1250.md).
